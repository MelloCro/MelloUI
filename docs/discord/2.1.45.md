**2.1.45: Your sounds, your way** 🔊

**Every sound is yours to change.** Custom Sounds has a new **Your Sounds** tab (`/mello` → Custom Sounds). Every sound it plays is listed there by a plain name, from Light Click and Item Picked Up to Whisper, Level Up and Rare Alert: 43 in all. Each one gets a list: keep MelloUI's sound, go back to the game's own, pick None, any other MelloUI sound, or any sound from SharedMedia. Press ▶ to hear it. Your picks work in every skin, Default included.

**Your own files.** Fancy an air horn for Rare Alert? Grab **MelloUI_MySounds.zip** from MelloUI's GitHub releases, drop your .ogg or .mp3 files in, add a line per sound and restart the game. The step-by-step is pinned in #faq, and the full guide is linked at the top of the tab. Your sounds show up in other addons that use SharedMedia too, and MelloUI updates never touch that folder.

**SharedMedia on the Addons page.** SharedMedia has its own card now: its fonts, textures and sounds in MelloUI's lists, with a switch like the others.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating; MelloUI_MySounds needs a full restart the first time. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
