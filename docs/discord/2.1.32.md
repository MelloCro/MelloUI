**2.1.32: SharedMedia, give it a try** 🧪

**SharedMedia support, as a test.** Fonts, bar textures and backgrounds from SharedMedia packs now show up in MelloUI's lists, also from packs that load after MelloUI. Try it with your packs and tell us how it goes; screenshots welcome.

**Tooltips get a Border choice.** On Look > Textures: Dark, the game's own, none, or a SharedMedia border. SharedMedia backgrounds work for tooltips, the chat and party and raid frames.
