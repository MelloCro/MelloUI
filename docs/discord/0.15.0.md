**The "Loud and Clear" update** 🔊

**Everybody talks.** A brand-new voice pack voices every quest, every greeting and even the books: letters, plaques and signs are read aloud by a narrator, the whole book, even while you walk on. Each NPC keeps one voice for all its lines, so the innkeeper sounds like the same innkeeper every time. It's a separate (big) download in two parts on the release page: unzip both into your AddOns folder, where they make one `MelloUI_VoicePack` folder, and tick it in the addon list. It replaces the old pack, so that one can go.

**A calmer settings window.** The Configurator was rebuilt: grouped pages, every option in exactly one place, flat buttons and sliders you can type a number into. Pick a frame or a window at the top of its page, copy settings from one to another, and watch your unit frames change in a live preview. Search jumps straight to the option and lights it up. Borders and backgrounds are now pictures right on each page.

**Edit Layout.** Unlock the Windows retires. Drag anything, scroll to resize, right-click for exact numbers, and it snaps to its neighbours with a gold guide line. Nothing sticks until you press Save, and a surprise pull only pauses it. Action bars hand you over to the game's Edit Mode and back. Try `/mello edit`.

**Gamepad players, it's fixed.** Last update's known issue is gone: Escape and the world map no longer freeze, and the "blocked" popup under Options is history. The Quest List's map marks sit out while the Gamepad UI is on.

**First things first.** Quests that need an item before the objective now send you to the item: Marla's Last Wish walks you to Samuel Fipps before the grave, with a "First: loot..." line in the tracker. The World Marker is a beacon from afar, lands as a pin within 100 yards and steps aside inside a quest area.

**Which stance am I in?** Never wonder again: your stance, form, auto attack, open bag, selected tab, anything that's on now wears a thick gold ring.

**Also:** elites, rares and bosses wear metal crests (gold crown, silver star, red-bronze skull) and nameplate levels sit on a dark disc; the minimap is back to its normal size with Width and Height sliders, and the Quest Tracker hangs right under it; the Quest Log and the Quest List finally look like siblings; 19 quests with "Test" in their names are back in the Quest List; and a plain `/mello` opens the settings again.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do). Using Mello's layout from before? Home offers Fit to this screen once. Did a "blocked" popup turn MelloUI off? Tick it again in the AddOns list.
