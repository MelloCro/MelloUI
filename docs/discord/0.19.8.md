**0.19.8: three updates in one** 🛡️

The roadmap had 0.19.6, 0.19.7 and 0.19.8 coming one by one. They're all here at once: action buttons, the whole border library, auto quests and the Cooldown Manager.

**New:**
- Action buttons light up in your colour scheme while you hold their key or click them. The icon turns red when your target is out of range, deep blue when you're short on mana, and grey when it can't be used (Action Bars > Bars).
- A whole border library (Look > Borders): new rows for raid frames, unit frames, the cast bar and the personal resource display, four new styles on every row, and the Single rail and Backdrop on buttons, auras and bars. Raid Border Placement puts the border on each raid frame, round each group, or both.
- The backdrop round your action bars, micro menu and bag bar can wear any border style, and Frame Backdrop puts stone behind the player, target and focus frames.
- Two new rings, Four diamond studs and Plain heavy ring, plus a Portrait Ring row for the unit frame portraits (gold, silver or red-bronze for elites, rares and bosses).
- Two new backgrounds everywhere you pick one: Brushed dark metal and Aged parchment.
- Auto Accept Quests and Auto Hand In (Quest List > List), both off until you switch them on. Reward choices wait for you, unless Take The Upgrade picks the best one; hold Shift to do it all yourself.
- Cooldown Manager: Centre Rows, Key Bindings on the icons, Light While Pressed, Count Size, and its own border. Ready alerts and hiding a single spell were on the roadmap too: the game's own Cooldown Manager settings already do both, so MelloUI leaves them there.

**Fixed:**
- No more errors on tooltips, nameplate names and the game menu button in the first seconds after logging in or a /reload.
- The micro menu's spacing no longer upsets the game's own micro buttons, and MelloUI keeps its hands off the game's frames in general.
- The experience and reputation bars keep their own border and dark groove inside an action bar backdrop.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game after updating: 0.19.8 brings new files and new art, which a /reload doesn't load.
