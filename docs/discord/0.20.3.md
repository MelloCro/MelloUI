**0.20.3: Old paper, new tricks** 📜

The new parchment from 0.20.1 stays the default, but some of you missed the old one, so now you choose.

**Parchment Style** (Look > Parchment):
- **Burnt paper:** the new one, warm paper with burnt edges, and curled corners on the big pages.
- **Classic vellum:** the old vellum with its painted brush-stroke edges, as before 0.20.1.
- It changes every parchment at once (tooltips, chat, whispers, the trackers, the meter, the character window, the dialogs, the quest log, quest and spell book pages), no /reload.

**Fixed:**
- Edit Layout's Minimap plate now covers the minimap you actually see (the frame, the zone name and Services), and the minimap goes all the way into a corner of the screen.
- Hovering a weapon buff (a shaman's imbue, an oil or a poison) gave an error instead of its tooltip.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
