**0.17.1: the "no more hiccups" update** 🩹

**Smooth walking again.** With a quest tracked, Route could freeze the game for a moment while you walked: its new mountain-aware routes checked the ground far too often. Routes are now worked out a little at a time, so they never stall the game, and the dots still go round the ridge. Standing at the edge of a slope or in a building no longer sends you the long way round by boat either.

**Swing timers.** A shot bar for Auto Shot and a melee bar for every swing (a thinner one for your off hand, and wands count too), in a cast bar look or a slim hairline. Off by default: switch them on under Frames and bars.

**Reskin off? Now it fits.** Playing with the painted reskin switched off, the widget column, Voice Over's widget, the reminders by your portrait and the race bar now take the game's own look, from its tooltip frame to its fonts. More of MelloUI follows in the next updates.

**Also:** raid-style party and raid frames get the damage meter's number on the right, beside the health (DPS in gold, HPS in green). Point at a frame for the full story.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
