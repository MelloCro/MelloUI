**2.1.34: The minimap stays put** 🌿

**Herb and ore scanners fixed.** If you use NodeSight (or a similar herb and ore addon), your minimap's frame could follow your cursor around as soon as Find Herbs or Find Minerals was on. Since Forever's last update, those addons scan the minimap by moving it under your cursor, hidden, to read each dot. MelloUI's frame, zone bar and clock went along with it and stayed visible. Now they hide with the map during each scan and come back right after.

**Housekeeping.** Coming from 2.1.32 or 2.1.33, a /reload is enough; from 2.1.31 or older, restart the game fully. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
