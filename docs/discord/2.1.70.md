**2.1.70: numbers your way, and Chinese that fits** 🔢

Thank you Wolvegrimm and 轻风细雨 for the requests and the careful reports!

**Nameplates.** Health Text Size makes the health numbers on the plates smaller (or bigger), so they stop filling a small plate. Percent Beside The Bar keeps the number in the middle of the bar and puts the percent beside it. In the Nameplate Designer the health numbers are now a part of their own: drag them anywhere on the plate, size them with the wheel, or hide them.

**Personal Display.** Percent Position has a new choice, Number On The Bars, Percent Beside: your actual health and power in the middle of the bars, the percents beside them. New Text Size and Text Colour sliders change all of its text.

**Map.** Only The Tracked Quests and Focused Quest Only now change the open map at once. Type /qlmap focus to see which quest Focused Quest Only is showing.

**Chinese.** Titles and headings were about a quarter too large, and lines could run into each other; they now take their right size. On some Chinese clients a few titles showed boxes instead of Chinese letters; they show the letters now.

**Also (2.1.69).** With a controller, the /mellobug report now names exactly where the game's controller navigation ran into MelloUI. If the game blocks an action while you play with a controller, send us your /mellobug report.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
