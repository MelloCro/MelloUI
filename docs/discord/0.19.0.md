**0.19.0: the "where did I put that?" update** 🎒

**Bags by Kind** (new, on by default):
- Your bags in one window, sorted by kind: what you just picked up, gear, quest items, consumables, junk, trade goods and the rest, each under its own heading.
- Small kinds share a row, so the window is barely bigger than before. All your empty slots fold into one slot with a count: drop an item on it and it finds a free slot.
- Click a heading to fold that kind away. Junk shows what it sells for, and at a vendor one click sells it all.
- Make it yours: turn kinds on or off, split Trade Goods into Cloth, Leather, Herbs and friends, and pick the columns, the slot size and the order. The new gear button on the bag window takes you straight to these settings.
- Miss the old bags? Switch it off on Windows > Bags.

**Healer Frames** (for everyone keeping the party alive):
- Incoming Heals: heals on their way show on the health bar, yours in teal, other healers' darker, shields striped. Forever's player, target and focus frames finally get them too.
- Debuff Glow: a friend with a removable debuff glows in its colour (Magic blue, Curse purple, Disease brown, Poison green). Choose every debuff, the ones your group can remove, or only the ones you can.
- Bar Background: pick what sits behind your health and power bars, and how solid it is.

**Fix:** the Discard button no longer shows a cog behind its bin.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
