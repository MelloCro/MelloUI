**2.1.41: Know where you stand** 🧭

**Coordinates, as you asked.** Switch on **Map Coordinates** (Map page) and the line under the map shows your coordinates, plus the mouse's while it's over the map, on the zone the line names. **Minimap Coordinates** (Minimap page) puts yours right under the minimap picture, at the end of the Services line, and turns gold with the mouse's spot while you hover the minimap. Both follow the game's own choice of tenths or whole numbers. Off by default, so switch them on in /mello.

**Dark Mode keeps its hands off words.** Gold text no longer darkens with Dark Mode: the MelloUI button in the Game Menu, the chat's headers and the map's quest counts stay bright; only frames and backgrounds darken.

**Housekeeping.** Coming from 2.1.40 or older, restart the game fully (a new file). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
