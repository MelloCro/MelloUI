**2.1.54: profiles that stay put, and borders that tell you what bit you** 🧩

**Profiles, once and for all.** Since 2.1.46 a logout quietly wrote the game's preset layout into your profile, so alts and loaded profiles ended up on Modern. Fixed: a profile now carries the Edit Mode layout you really use, and every character keeps it in a layout of its own, so moving a frame on your alt no longer rearranges your main. A new character gets its copied profile's layout at its first login, and a loaded profile lands whole (one /reload when MelloUI asks for it).

**Buffs & Debuffs.** Debuff borders wear their type's colour: Magic blue, Curse purple, Disease orange, Poison green, Bleed red, on your icons, the target's and, new, enemy nameplates, with a colour wheel for each on Look > Colours. **Timer Position** puts the time under, above or on the icon, and it reads 60m, not 60min (thanks for asking, BaQstein).

**Leveling guides.** A guide's heading on the Quest Tracker has **Back** and **Skip** arrows, with RestedXP, Zygor, Azeroth Pilot Reloaded, Follow The Arrow and Polestar. RestedXP can show its next steps and real item links, and Zygor's steps move on by themselves again.

**Level Up, dungeons, sounds.** The Level Up card is remade: a spark, a gold seal, your new spells landing one by one. All nine dungeons have Santiago Reyes' maps, and a dungeon's door on the map opens its page. With Custom Sounds on, the Spellbook and its friends are no longer silent, and windows and bags get sound rows of their own.
