**2.1.55: grab the controller, pick your role, and know what bites** 🎮

**Controller Forever.** MelloUI now plays along with it. Its buttons and binds are its own, MelloUI's gamepad bars stay out of the way, and where MelloUI already has the part (unit frames, party frames, minimap, chat, meter, bags) its version steps back. Open a MelloUI window and the D-pad, A and B walk it. Its healer mode highlights the member you pick on MelloUI's party frames. It has a card with a switch on the Addons page.

**Quest Tracker and whispers on the pad.** A new **Quest Tracker** action steps into the tracker: the D-pad walks your quests, A clicks, X uses the quest's item, Y opens its menu, B leaves. **Next Quest** marks the next one without taking the pad from you. The whisper window takes the D-pad too. Both actions are in Key Bindings.

**Role Picker.** Walk into a dungeon in a group and your class's roles drop in above your character. One click and it is yours. Tick Remember and the next dungeons set it by themselves.

**Levels on the map.** Three new switches on the map's Layers page, all off until you want them: zone level ranges under each name, creature levels as you zoom into a zone (both need Questie), and fishing skill badges on coasts, lakes and rivers. The minimap shows the ones near you.

**Fixed.** The Addons page opens again with MaxDPS installed (thanks for the hunt, Slayristo).

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
