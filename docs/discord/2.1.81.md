**2.1.81: controller fixes, and quests missing from the map** 🎮

**Controller.** If the game told you "MelloUI has been blocked" and your controller's buttons stopped answering, this one is for you. Thank you for the /mellobug reports!
- With a controller, a click on a name or a link in MelloUI's chat no longer opens the game's menu, its chat line or its item window. Opened from MelloUI, those could leave the controller's buttons blocked. A link still shows its tooltip under the pointer; whispers and the player menu go through the game's own chat button.
- MelloUI's own buttons inside the game's windows (Discard on the loot window and the bags, the half on the split box, the recipients on the mail window) are skipped by the controller's cursor now. The mouse still works on them.
- Still blocked? Please send your /mellobug report again: it now notes more of what happened before the block.

**Quest marks without Questie (2.1.80).** Some quests in your log had no mark or area on MelloUI's map and the minimap. Two things were wrong:
- MelloUI looked for each quest on one map only. It now reads the game's quest places from every zone, and again when they arrive after a login or a new quest.
- The game only hands out all quest places while its option Show Quest POIs & Blobs (Options > Preset) is on, and MelloUI itself turned that option off to hide the game's blue markers. MelloUI now keeps it on while its quest marks are on. Hide The Game's Quest Areas (Quest List > Map) still hides the game's own markers and areas on the game's map. Your own setting comes back when you turn MelloUI's quest marks off.

With Questie, the same fills in the quests Questie has no places for. Still missing one? Type /qlmap game: it lists what the game gives for each quest in your log.
