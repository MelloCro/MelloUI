**1.2.1: You said, we fixed** 🔧

A small update made of your reports. Thank you to everyone who wrote in.

**Fixed:**
- **Stance changes and lag:** after learning a new stance or form, a fight could drop to a few frames a second until you restarted. The game now recovers by itself.
- **"Screen too small":** the installer could call your screen too small for Mello's layout at any UI scale, ultrawides included. When it is some of your own settings that don't fit, it now says so.
- **Aura spam:** an aura that keeps coming and going, like a warlock's Demonic Knowledge as the pet moves in and out of range, is announced once, not every few seconds.
- **Pet names (Oldschool):** popups that ask you to type, like renaming your hunter pet, show their text box again.
- The Dungeon Info button on the map is as sharp as the other icons.

**Housekeeping.** A new file this time: restart the game after updating, not just /reload. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
