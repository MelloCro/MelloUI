**2.0.0: Pick your look** 🎨

A big one: MelloUI dresses three ways now, and levelling up finally throws a little party.

**Three skins, one switch** (Look > General): **Default** puts MelloUI's windows, bags, chat, tracker, minimap and party frames in the game's own look, **Modern** is MelloUI's own, **Oldschool** is the painted look (MelloUI Oldschool download). Nothing moves when you switch.

**New installer:** your skin first, then each part step by step with real pictures. It fits MelloUI to your screen's shape, ultrawide too.

**New configurator:** one painted window per skin, plain tabs, options two to a line, and Dark Mode reaching everything. Parts of its design come from EllesmereUI, with its author's kind permission. Thank you!

**Edit Layout:** a dock along the top, every Edit Mode part with its settings in its box, and moved frames keep their screen edges when your window changes shape.

**Bigger moments:** bolder heal and cooldown effects, buffs landing on your group in their school's colour, a threat halo, and the **Level Up Card**: what each level gave you and the spells waiting at your trainer. Try `/mello player level`.

**Also:** damage meter numbers in a tab on each frame, one tidy group for every addon's minimap button, larger and sharper party portraits, and Numbers Over Enemies works again.

**Housekeeping.** New files this time: restart the game fully after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
