**2.1.43: RareScanner, and a calmer map** 🗺️

**RareScanner, in MelloUI's look.** Got RareScanner installed? Its rares, treasures and events now show on MelloUI's map and minimap as MelloUI icons on a plate, and the rim tells you where you stand: gold when you've just seen one, red when you haven't found it yet, muted once you have. Hover one for its name, click it to drop your pin there. The rares it finds also pop up in MelloUI's **Rare Alert** with where they are, so RareScanner's own alert button is switched off once (turn it back on in RareScanner if you want both). Thanks, whatever_69!

**Fewer icons with Questie.** Questie's swords and sacks no longer pile up on top of MelloUI's quest areas, whatever icons you picked in Questie's options. Its flight masters stay hidden too while the map's Flight masters layer shows MelloUI's own.

**One outline instead of ten rings.** Quest areas that touch now show as one gold outline, on the map, the game's map and the minimap. Every objective keeps its marks where it's done: hover a mark and its own area lights up inside. Minimap marks now lift and light their area when you hover them, just like on the map.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (a new file). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
