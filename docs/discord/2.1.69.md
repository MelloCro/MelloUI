**2.1.69: a sharper bug report for controller players** 🎮

A small one, for the controller players who send us their /mellobug reports. Thank you, they are how we track these down!

**Blocked actions report (/mellobug).** With a controller, the report now also follows the game's own controller navigation as it looks through a window for its buttons, and names the part of the window where things first went wrong. If the game ever blocks an action while you play with a controller (a line like "MelloUI forbidden by the game: InteractUnit()"), type /mellobug, copy the report and send it to us on Discord or GitHub.
