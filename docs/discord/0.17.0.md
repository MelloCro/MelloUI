**The "In The Thick Of It" update** ⚔️

**Your fights, in MelloUI's look.** Combat Text gets three styles for the hits, heals and procs around you (Lanes, Feed or Classic), and Your Damage pops your numbers at each enemy while you play solo: rising, fanning out or stacking. Crits in gold, spells in violet.

**A damage meter that stays out of your way.** Your numbers on your frame and each party frame, a race bar with class medallions in group fights, a summary after every fight and a Fight History for the whole session. No windows to babysit.

**Fade what you don't need.** The new Fader page: every frame, bar, the chat and the minimap can show Always, In Combat or On Mouseover. Or press Fade Everything and let your UI vanish between pulls.

**Try it before you pull.** Preview (top of the settings window) plays a short made-up fight, solo, in a party or one part at a time, so you can see your setup in action without hunting a boar first.

**Also:** Route walks round mountains instead of over them, reminders for missing buffs and poisons (a click puts them back), a quest item widget, a heads-up for tanks when the healer is drinking, Gains counts your gold, the chat's own buttons are back, and fights no longer cost you frames.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
