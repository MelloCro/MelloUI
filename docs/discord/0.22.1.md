**0.22.1: Little touches** ✨

A small update with five more little animations, so your XP, money, bags and Services row show you what just changed.

**New:**
- **XP Gain:** the stretch of the experience bar you just earned lights up as a light runs along it to the new end.
- **Rolling Money:** the bag window's money rolls to its new sum, warm going up, red going down. Coins you loot with your bags open fly in as coins and count it up.
- **Bags Full Warning:** with your bags full, the Empty group shows a 0 slot, and it shakes and flashes red when the game says "Inventory is full." Bags closed? The backpack button flashes instead.
- **Items Glide:** when the bag window sorts again, items glide to their new slots and new ones fade in.
- **Trays Slide Out:** the Services row's groups open like a drawer out of the minimap column, and slide back in when they close.

Each one has its own switch (Windows > Bags, Minimap > Services Bar, Bars & Meters > XP & Reputation), and Reduce Motion turns them all off. Want a look without waiting for a mob? /mello player xp, /mello bags money and /mello bags full.

**Housekeeping.** Nothing to restart beyond the usual update. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
