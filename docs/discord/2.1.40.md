**2.1.40: Your requests, delivered** 📬

**One bag view.** A new option, **Reagent Bag With The Bags** (Windows › Bags), puts your Reagent Bag's items in with the rest of your bags, its slots marked in lime, its free slots counted in an Empty slot of their own next to your normal and special bags. No more flipping between tabs. Thanks, TribalInstincts!

**A tidier chat.** The short channel tags (G, P, R ...) now wear the game's own chat colours, and the Minimal Chat View's headers show just the letter. A new **Chat Timestamps** option sets the game's own timestamps: pick None to hide them. And the whisper windows have a **Copy** button: the whole conversation in a window, links and long messages included, ready for Ctrl+C.

**The map opens where you are.** The first time you open the map after logging in, it shows your current zone (a city's own map when you're in one) instead of the whole world. Thanks, Nixxen! And Krol'dok Stronghold's entrance now shows in the Riverglades, with Route to take you there. Thanks, Tereith!

**Housekeeping.** Coming from 2.1.39, a /reload is enough; from 2.1.38 or older, restart the game fully. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
