**2.1.17: Glow up** ✨

**Glowing Bars:** it now lights MelloUI's own action bars and stance bar in every skin, instead of the hidden game bars underneath.

**Keybind Mode:** a key you bind in place of a default one, like those on action bar 1, now stays after you log in again.

**Battlegrounds:** MelloUI's map now opens on the battleground's own map, with you and your group on it.

**Guide addons:** a new Follow Guide Addons switch (Route > General) turns off showing RestedXP's and other guides' steps in the Quest Tracker.

**Dead players:** their frames no longer keep a debuff or threat glow.

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
