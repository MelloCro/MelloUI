**2.1.56: Questie's quest marks, bigger and easier to click** 🗺️

**Map.** With Questie, its quest ! and ? on MelloUI's map are now as big as the map's other marks, not Questie's own small size, and they follow the map's Icon Size slider. A click near one starts the route there. Before, only a click on the small icon itself counted, and a near miss zoomed the map in.

**Questie, any version.** MelloUI now reads Questie's database whatever its version, older or newer. Before, some versions could leave MelloUI acting as if Questie was not installed.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
