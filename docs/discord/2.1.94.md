**2.1.94: bag items work again (thanks ChaosieKinder!)** 🎒

**Bags.** If you use MelloUI's action bars, right-clicking items in your bags (a sleeping bag, a recipe) could get blocked with "MelloUI tried to call UseContainerItem()", usually after a trip to the bank. Opening the bags from MelloUI's bag bar was the cause. The bag bar now opens them through the game's own buttons, so that's gone. ChaosieKinder tracked it down and sent the fix, thank you!
