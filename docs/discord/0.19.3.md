**0.19.3: the "no more ghost quests" update** 🗺️

**Fixed:**
- The Quest List's map stops sending you to quest givers who have nothing for you. A yellow "!" now means the quest is really there: quests that need an earlier quest of their chain, a profession or a reputation wait until you have it (the list's tooltip tells you what's missing), quests for other races, quests you've outlevelled and the other choice of an either-or quest are left out, and holiday quests only show on the map while they're in your log or the list is set to Events.

**New:**
- The Quest List now learns from the quest givers themselves. Talk to one, and if a quest the list expected isn't on offer, its "!" disappears for that character (the tooltip says why). It gets checked again when you level up, learn a profession, hand in a quest to that giver or talk to them again.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game after updating: a /reload isn't enough this time, since the Quest List keeps what it learns about givers in a new save file per character.
