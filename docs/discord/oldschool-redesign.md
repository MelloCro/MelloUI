**Oldschool gets new frames, in three sets** 🛡️

The Oldschool look's **unit frames**, **cast bar**, **Personal Resource Display**, **nameplates** and **chat** have been redrawn from scratch, and you get to pick the style:

- **Gilded Stone:** dark carved stone edged in aged gold, with a ruby on the portrait ring. The closest match to the Oldschool windows.
- **Forged Iron:** blackened iron with brass rivets and a knotwork boss on the ring. The calmest of the three.
- **Carved Wood:** dark walnut with a gold inlay and a leaf wreath round the portrait.

**What's different:**
- **One piece of art per frame** instead of many small border pieces joined together. Each one stretches cleanly to whatever size you give your frames, nameplates or chat, and the corner ornaments always stay whole.
- **Your target glows.** Its nameplate and frames get a soft glow in the shape of their frame, and frames glow under your mouse too.
- **The level sits in a badge** made for each set, and the nameplates keep the same size as in the Modern look.
- **One choice for all of it:** Oldschool > Borders > Frame Set. Switching is live, no reload.

The pictures are straight from the game. Coming in the next update. Nothing changes for Modern or Default, and your settings and layouts stay as they are.

**Which one are you picking?** 🪨 ⚔️ 🌳
