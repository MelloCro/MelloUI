**2.1.53: signposts for the towns, and nameplates that know about threat** 🗺️

**Where is the auctioneer?** With the addon HandyNotes: NPCs (Forever) installed, MelloUI's map and minimap now mark the auction house, the bank, riding trainers, mount sellers, innkeepers, mailboxes and the trainers of your own class and professions, all in MelloUI's own icons. A new **HandyNotes** group on the map's layers page has a switch for every kind of place. Repair, vendors and the rest start off, so Orgrimmar stays a city and not a sticker album. And only one addon draws a kind of place: no more two inns on one doorstep.

**Nameplates.** The cast bar in a fight now stands exactly where the Nameplate Designer shows it, and it has a tab of its own with a **Height** slider. New: **Threat Colours By Role** colours an enemy's health bar in group fights by its threat on you, tank or not, with a colour wheel for each of the three colours.

**Party and raid frames.** A group you join mid-fight shows up at once instead of after the fight. The **Lockouts** group works now: Weakened Soul after your shield, Forbearance after your blessing. And the starting indicators no longer sit on the name, the health text or the raid mark.

**Loot rolls.** Up to four rolls show at once, each on its own row, and hovering one tells you who already rolled Need, Greed or Pass, and who is still thinking about it (thanks for asking, Nixxen).

**Guides and trackers.** Zygor's steps now show on the Quest Tracker and Route follows them, no slash command needed. Questie's tracker follows the Quest Tracker page's width, height and text sizes, and a guide's steps no longer vanish when your quest log is empty.

**Two screens.** With Offhand, Quest Moments now play on your game screen, not in the gap between your monitors.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
