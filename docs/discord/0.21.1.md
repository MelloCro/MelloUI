**0.21.1: Your kinds, your bank** 🎒

You asked for your own categories in Bags by Kind and for the bank to join in. Both are here.

**Your own kinds:**
- Windows > Bags > Kinds > **Add kind**. Drop items on its + slot, or give it a rule: an item type and subtype, a word in the name, a quality (Rare or better, say). It takes its items before the game's kinds, the higher on the list first.
- **Rename any kind**, the game's and your own: double-click its name, or open its settings with the cog on its row.
- **Split By Type** for any kind: Consumable into Potion, Elixir, Food & Drink and so on, each with its own heading. Split Trade Goods is now Trade Goods' own Split By Type; your setting carried over.

**Bank by Kind (new, on):**
- Your bank in the same kinds as your bags, inside the bank window: your order, lines, groups and your own kinds, every bank page at once, the empty slots folded into one.
- Deposit and take out as always: right-click or drag. The window grows to fit.
- Rather have the game's grid? Windows > Bank > Bank by Kind.

**Housekeeping.** Nothing to restart beyond the usual update. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
