**0.19.4: the big settings tidy-up** 🧹

**New:**
- Nameplates: elites, rares, rare elites and bosses get a mark of their own, a metal line over the name with a crest in the middle (gold and winged for elites, a silver star for rares, red-bronze scrollwork round a skull for bosses). Switch it with Elite and Rare Marks.
- Chat: the Dark background is now a clean dark panel behind the chat, its tabs and the input box. No Dark Mode needed.
- Settings that do nothing in your setup are now dimmed, with a line saying what turns them on.

**Changed:**
- Fonts: Font Styles no longer change text that has its own size slider. Your current look is kept.
- The character window's parchment is one choice: Off, Right pane or Whole window.
- A new bar texture reaches the tooltip, the swing timers and the meter's race bar at once. The swing timers now follow the Cast Bars texture.
- Bags by Kind uses the Window Background you pick, and Tooltip Scale also sizes comparison tooltips.
- A new route destination plays one sound instead of two.
- Removed a few switches that never worked on Forever: PvP Flag Icon and Game Text Around You.

**Fixed:** class-coloured health bars turning NPC plates green, Frame Art Opacity, Custom Sounds' Equipment and Vendor sounds, the Ready Check alert sound, an error when switching Bags by Kind off, and more.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game after updating: the new nameplate marks are new art, which a /reload doesn't load.
