**2.1.86: Healing Numbers that look like healing** 💚

**Healing Numbers.** In 2.1.85 a heal on a friend popped in like a hit, and a crit heal even got the gold crit burst, so it looked like your friend was taking damage. Now a heal has a + before its number, swells in softly and floats up with a soft light in the healing colour. A crit heal is bigger, with a ring of that light round it. No flash, no shake.

**Weapon swings in groups.** After your first heal, the numbers of your weapon swings could go missing in a group. Fixed.
