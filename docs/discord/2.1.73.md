**2.1.73: a preview for your Personal Display, and battlegrounds you can read** 🗺️

Thank you Nixxen for the battleground request!

**Personal Display.** The Personal tab of Unit & Group Frames has its own preview now, like Player and the nameplates: your bars, the cast bar, the shield column, the swing badge, the big cooldowns and the text, all at once and in the look your display wears. Click a part to open its settings. Drag the cast bar, the text, the swing badge or the big cooldowns somewhere else and your display follows; the wheel sizes the badge and the cooldowns. Right-click a part and pick Default place to put it back.

**Battleground map.** Inside a battleground, the map now shows its bases, towers, graveyards and camps in the game's own icons, changing with who holds them: Alliance, Horde, contested or nobody. Hover one for its name and state. No more guessing which flag is whose.
