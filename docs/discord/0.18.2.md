**0.18.2: the "bags full? not any more" update** 🗑️

**Discard (a player's request).** A new button left of Sort on your bag window frees a slot by throwing away your cheapest grey or white junk. At a vendor it sells it instead, so nothing is lost. It never touches white gear, food, drink, potions, scrolls, conjured items, crafting materials, reagents, ammunition, quest items, keys or anything on your Restock list, and never a stack worth 1 gold or more (you can change that limit). A white item asks first. Point at the button to see what would go.

**On the loot window too.** The same button sits under the loot list, and only makes room when what you're looting is worth more than what it would throw away.

**Fixes:**
- Party frame names no longer show up twice and garbled.
- The line you type in chat starts at the left again instead of in the middle.
- The tooltip at your cursor fades out like the game's own, so you can still read it for a moment.
- The check marks next to a creature's quest objectives show again on the tooltip's parchment.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
