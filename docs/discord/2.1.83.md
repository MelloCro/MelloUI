**2.1.83: friendly nameplates get their own settings** 🛡️

Thank you TheLastAlliance for the report!

**Three kinds of nameplate.** The Nameplates designer now has Enemies, Friendly NPCs and Friendly players, each with its own preview, places and sizes. Before, what you set under Enemies (a moved name, its size, the plate's size, the health numbers, the cast bar) was laid on friendly plates too.

**Heads up.** Friendly plates start at the game's own layout. If you had moved or resized parts, your friendly plates go back to it with this update: set them in their own tab.

**Hide At Full Health.** New, for friendly plates: at full health only the name shows, and the health bar appears as soon as they are hurt. Nameplates > Friendly NPCs or Friendly players > Health bar. Off by default.

This one is fresh, so tell us how it behaves for you, in fights too.
