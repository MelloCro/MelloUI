**1.1.0: Now in your language** 🌍

MelloUI learns seven languages, and Route finally stops taking the scenic tour.

**New:**
- **Languages:** the settings window and the installer speak German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese and Korean. They start in your game's language; the Language dropdown at the top left switches it. It's a first draft: spot a clumsy line? Fix it on MelloUI's CurseForge translation page.
- **Route over the real ground:** Route now walks the game's own walkable ground: stairs, ramps, bridges, the streets of Ironforge and Undercity, and boats and zeppelins picked by their real wait and ride time. No more circles round a hill.
- **Forever's new dungeons on the map:** Dalaran (each side sees its own door), the Excavation Site in the Wetlands, the Ruins of Lordaeron and the Hall of Thanes, plus the coming Drowned City and Shaper's Terrace. Route takes you to your side's entrance.
- **Game menu:** MelloUI's button under Escape is a gold pill with the badge now, hard to miss.

**Fixed:**
- A dead target no longer shows a 0 on top of "Dead".
- The cooldown spiral no longer sweeps over the borders of buffs and debuffs; dispel colours stay whole.

**Housekeeping.** New files this time: restart the game after updating, not just /reload. Updating by hand? Unzip every folder from the zip; **MelloUI_Languages** is new among them. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
