**2.1.85: Combat Text redone** 💥

**Your damage.** Combat Damage Style (it was Your Damage) has three new looks: Pop, Slam and Stamp. Hits land harder and are gone sooner, each look has its own crit effect, spell hits are in their school's colour, and damage over time ticks sit small in a lane of their own. Number Outline is new too: Thick, Thin or None. If you used Rise, Beside or Fan you now have Pop, and Stack became Stamp.

**In groups (a test).** MelloUI now shows your own hits in its look in a group too, told apart from everyone else's by the combat log. It needs the Combat Log's filter on My actions, and your pet's hits are not shown in a group. Try it and tell us how it goes. Combat Damage In Groups turns it off.

**Healing Numbers.** With a Combat Damage Style picked, your heals show in the same look at the nameplate of the friend you heal. It needs friendly nameplates shown. In dungeons and raids you get the game's own healing numbers, as before.

**Around your character.** Pop, Slam and Stamp are there for the text around you as well. Damage Taken, Healing, Procs and Resources each have their own area, and Edit Position on the Combat Text tab lets you drag them and size their text.

**Where things went.** Combat Text Style is now Personal Combat Text Style, and both text sizes are on the Combat Text tab (Quality of Life).

**Oldschool.** The Borders tab is shorter: two rows that no longer changed anything are gone.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
