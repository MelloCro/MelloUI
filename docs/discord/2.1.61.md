**2.1.61: loot rolls you can read, Polestar in the tracker, druid bars on your frame** 🎲

**Loot rolls.** Group loot rolls have a window of their own in every look: what dropped, everyone's pick and number, your Need, Greed and Pass, and a raid's forty in a scrolling table. A favourite from your Dungeon Info list stands out, and the item flies to the winner's frame. Under Master Loot it shows what the loot master hands out, and the loot master gets Roll and Assign for each item on the boss.

**Polestar in the Quest Tracker.** Sent in by Madspike, thank you. Click a goal line or the guide's title to follow that quest. The guide's step shows the distance to its spot and its quest item button, a goal lights up once when it is done, and a tab button switches between Polestar's open guides. Step numbers match Polestar's window, and the guide section no longer vanishes on some steps.

**Druid Forms on the player frame.** Asked for here on Discord: a druid's other two powers now sit in one more row under the power bar, split in two, each with its number (the numbers follow Bar Values). On the Personal Display, health, power and that row share one border with separators, in every look.

**Map.** A rare is marked by its skull alone. With GatherMate2, its herbs, veins, fishing pools and treasures wear MelloUI's own icons, on the map and the minimap.

**Fixes.** A priest's Mind Control no longer hides the pet bar with the controlled creature's abilities. A name setting is no longer written during a fight after a /reload. Controller players: the blocked actions report (/mellobug) says more about the cause in the Gamepad UI now, so if you still get blocked actions with a pad, please send the report again after updating.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
