**2.1.1: Room to breathe** 🧹

A tidy-up of the settings window, one indicator layout for all your alts, and a Stealth button that actually stealths.

**The settings window:** frame previews stand on the left with room around them, and the Player preview is *you* now: your name, level, class colour and portrait. Search understands how people talk (color, hp, castbar, hotkey ...), Escape backs out one step at a time, and settings kept on another page show as small chips with a way back. A new Settings Window Scale on Look sizes the window, and helper texts are full size. Dungeon Info moved in with the Map.

**Spell groups for every class:** one party and raid indicator layout for all your characters, every group holding every class's spells, and your class's own tweaks on top. Your old per-character layouts are kept: Copy from brings them back. The fake raid now comes in 5, 10, 25 or 40.

**Edit Layout:** Grid, Snap, Dim and Coordinates live in one View menu.

**Fixed:** clicking Stealth, a stance, a form or a pet action on MelloUI's bars only lit the button up. Rogues, sorry, you were seen. Holy Nova, Prayer of Mending, Holy Shock, Earth Shield and Healing Rain show their heal effects at every rank.

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
