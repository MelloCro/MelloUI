**1.2.3: No more waiting** 🧭

Route gets quicker and lighter, and your Swing Badge finally strikes when you do.

**Faster:**
- **Service buttons:** Repair, Innkeeper, Flight Master and the rest route to the nearest one at once again. Since 1.1.0 a click could take several seconds.
- **The other continent:** routes across the sea are found faster, and while one is found you see "Loading navigation..." with its bar, like the first route of a session.
- **Hard-to-reach spots:** a route to a place the roads don't reach (inside a building, on a ledge, behind a fence) starts at once instead of after several seconds.
- **Lighter:** Route throws away about half the memory while its roads go in, and far less while it finds routes, so MelloUI's memory reading swings much less.

**Fixed:**
- After one route from a spot the roads don't reach, routes to the other continent could keep failing until you learned a new flight point.
- The **Swing Badge** strikes when your blow lands, not when the swing starts, and a crit or a parry shows on the blow it belongs to.
- An error in fights from the Personal Display's Big Cooldowns.

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
