**2.1.72: every ore its own icon, and the plates take more orders** ⛏️

Thank you Wolvegrimm for another round of nameplate requests!

**Nameplate Designer.** The health number and the percent are two parts now: drag each one anywhere on the plate, hide either, and give the percent its own size with Percent Size. Name Size is new too (Nameplates > Enemies > Name), and the wheel over a name in the Designer sets it. Fixed: with Percent Beside The Bar, the swing badge on your target's plate no longer covers the percent.

**GatherMate2.** Every ore and every herb now has its own MelloUI icon on the map and the minimap. Copper, tin, iron, mithril, thorium and the rest each wear their own colour, and all 30 herbs are drawn as themselves, from Peacebloom to Black Lotus. The Oldschool look has its own painted set; the Default look keeps GatherMate2's own pictures. No more squinting at a map full of identical leaves.

**Lueur.** Using Lueur for its glow on the node you walk up to? While its glow is on, Loot Sparkles' glints over ore, herbs and quest objects step aside and leave the game's soft targeting settings to it, so the two stop fighting over them. The quest item sparkle stays. It has its own card and switch on the Addons page.

**Housekeeping.** This one needs a full restart of the game after updating (new icons). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
