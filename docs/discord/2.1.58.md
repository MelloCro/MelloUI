**2.1.58: Oldschool's new frames, controller polish** 🎮

**Oldschool.** The unit frames, the cast bar, the Personal Display, the nameplates and the chat have new frames, in three sets to pick from: Gilded Stone, Forged Iron and Carved Wood (Oldschool > Borders > Frame Set). Each frame is one painting that stretches to any size with its ornaments whole. Your target glows in the shape of its frame, and elites, rares and bosses wear their own ring.

**Controller.** From a player's feedback: a mouse click on the MelloUI button under the Game Menu closes the menu. The D-pad skips a window's close button and its tabs (B closes, LB / RB switch tabs). The game's yellow pointer no longer shows over MelloUI's windows. On the map the D-pad reaches the buttons beside the quest list, Marker on your pin removes it and the crosshair is faster. Picking Chat on the ring menu gives MelloUI's chat the controller: LB / RB switch tabs, the D-pad or the right stick scrolls.

**Discard.** Answering Discard on its question now throws a white item away. Before, the game refused it every time.

**Tooltips.** With Questie and Polestar, a creature's quest objectives are no longer listed twice. Questie's lines are the ones.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
