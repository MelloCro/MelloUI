**2.1.92: a round of fixes, plus spirit healers on the map** 🪦

**Spirit healers.** They're on MelloUI's map and the minimap now (Map Layers > Travel > Spirit healers). The spots come from Questie, or from the game where it knows them.

**Rares and chests.** There's a Rares switch in Map Layers under HandyNotes, so you can hide the rares a HandyNotes plugin puts on your map. Chests lost their round plate and look like the rares do: just the chest. Opened ones are faded.

**Flight masters.** With HandyNotes: WoW Forever, every flight master showed up twice. Now only MelloUI's icon is there while Flight masters is on.

**Chat.** In the Minimal chat view, clicking the language name on your typing line finally opens the menu, so you can switch to Orcish or Darnassian. The view's scroll bar can be dragged again too.

**Quest log.** In Oldschool with MelloUI's map off, a quest you found with the search box wouldn't open, and it crept down a bit with every click. Fixed.

**Lua errors at login.** Two of them are gone: one from Buffs & Debuffs ("Anchoring disallowed") and one from the game's party frames ("maximum number of mask textures").

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
