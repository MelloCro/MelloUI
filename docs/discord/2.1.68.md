**2.1.68: five of your requests, one update** 🗺️

You asked on Discord, we built it. Thank you Lively, Wolvegrimm, Slayristo, Nixxen and inshape!

**Focused Quest Only.** Turn it on under Quest List > Map, follow a quest from the Quest Tracker, and the map and the minimap show only that one, as the game's own map does. Follow none and every quest comes back.

**Loot All Key.** Turn it on under Windows > Loot windows: open a corpse, see what it has, then press your Interact key (or a key you pick) to take it all. In a fight the key keeps its normal job until the fight ends. Group and master loot are respected.

**Click Through.** For Clique players: the Personal Display can now let clicks and camera drags pass through (Unit & Group Frames > Personal), so turning the camera no longer casts a heal.

**Move it in Edit Layout.** The totem and minion timers (a hunter's hawks, a shaman's totems) and the battleground and zone objectives at the top of the screen are now elements you can move and size, and they keep their place in fights.

**Housekeeping.** This one needs a full restart of the game after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
