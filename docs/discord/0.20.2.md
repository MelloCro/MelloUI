**0.20.2: Drag everything** 🧲

Edit Layout now moves the game's own frames too, backdrops listen to their switches, and a couple of unit frame fixes.

**Edit Layout:**
- Edit Layout moves everything the game's Edit Mode places: the unit frames, the cast bar, the party and raid frames, every action bar, the stance, pet, possess and totem bars and the rest. Drag them on their plates like MelloUI's own, snapping included.
- Their places are saved into your active Edit Mode layout as well, so Edit Mode shows the same after a /reload. Using one of the game's presets (Modern, Classic)? MelloUI asks once and makes a copy. Their size and other settings stay in Edit Mode.
- Frames that only show at times (the cast bar, target, focus, pet frame, stance, pet and totem bars) get their plate even while hidden, so you can place them any time.
- Group Frames: the raid has its own spacing now (Raid Spacing). Right-click the party or raid frames in Edit Layout to set the room between them.

**Action Bars > Backdrops:**
- Each switch now decides its own bar: on, the bar gets its backdrop and merges with the other switched-on bars next to it; off, no backdrop and no merging. Had a bar off beside one that was on? Switch it on to keep it inside.
- The Totem Bar is on the list.

**Unit frames:**
- The PvP icon lost its circle and sits on top of the level ring.
- Fixed: a target too high to tell shows its skull on the level ring again.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
