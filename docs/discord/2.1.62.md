**2.1.62: rows on the race bar, a mail list beside Send Mail, more French** ✉️

Most of this one was sent in by players again. Thank you, Glockta-code and woofix.

**Race bar in rows.** Race Bar Shows has a new choice, Your Pick: tick Damage, Healing and Threat, and each gets a row of its own, one under the other. Threat shows in a group or with your pet out, and its line turns from yellow to red as you get close to pulling. Auto stays the default and stays one row, so nothing changes until you pick (Bars & Meters > Damage Meter > Behaviour).

**Mail Recipients.** The small button beside the To field now opens a list beside the Send Mail window in place of its menu: your characters on top, your Battle.net friends under them, each friend unfolding their characters in place. A second click closes it, and it stays open or closed as you left it. Also fixed: a friend's character on another layer of your own server counted as being on another realm, and the letter got an address the game refused.

**French.** Edit Layout's controls, the Quest List's filters, Follow The Arrow's menu, Voice Over's buttons, the Stats tooltip and the boat route markers are translated. A text drawn very early at login could also lock MelloUI to English for that session; that is fixed for every language.

**Oldschool.** On the party frames the portrait's ring was drawn across the level badge. The badge stands over the ring now.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
