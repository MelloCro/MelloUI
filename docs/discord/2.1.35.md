**2.1.35: Buffs stay home** 🌿

**Minimap scanners, part two.** With a herb or ore addon like NodeSight scanning your minimap, the buffs beside your minimap could jump along with it and end up stuck at the top edge of your screen. They now stay where they belong during every scan, and so do the Quest Tracker and the other things lined up by the minimap. And if your minimap went missing after a scan, it now always comes back.

**About the small icons.** The little minimap icons you may still see near your cursor during a scan (a flight master, a town service) are the game's own minimap icons, which the scanning addon leaves visible. If they bother you, let that addon's author know.

**Housekeeping.** Coming from 2.1.32 or newer, a /reload is enough; from 2.1.31 or older, restart the game fully. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
