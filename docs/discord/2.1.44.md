**2.1.44: MelloUI and your other addons** 🧩

**A new Addons page.** Open /mello and you'll find **Addons** right under Profiles. It shows how MelloUI works with the other addons you run:

- **Installed:** a card for each addon you have that MelloUI works with. It shows whether everything works, what MelloUI does with it, buttons straight to its settings, and anything MelloUI switched off in it once, with a **Switch back on** button. If an addon update ever changes something MelloUI relies on, the card says so and only that part switches itself off.
- **Use with MelloUI:** a switch on each card. Turn it off and MelloUI leaves that addon completely alone after a /reload.
- **Compatibility:** every addon MelloUI works with, installed or not (Questie, RareScanner, RestedXP, Zygor, Polestar, TomTom, GatherMate2, HandyNotes, NodeSight, Lueur and Clique), each with its link to copy. `/mello compatibility` opens it directly.
- **Who does what:** for the jobs two addons could both do (the quest tracker, rare alerts, auto-accepting quests, repairing...), which one does each right now.

Want MelloUI to work with an addon that isn't on the list? Ask in the addon requests channel; the link is on the Compatibility tab too.

**Translations.** The new page is in all 7 languages, and woofix's French translations for the last updates are in. Merci, woofix!

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
