**2.1.96: MelloUI now works with Spoken** 🎙️

**Spoken.** If you use Spoken (the voice-over addon for quests, books, zones and bosses), MelloUI plays nice with it now. While Spoken is reading your quests, MelloUI's own Voice Over stays quiet, so you never hear two voices at once.

**Spoken in MelloUI's look.** Spoken can show its lines in MelloUI's Voice Over widget: the speaker's face, their name, the ring as the line plays, and the buttons. Spoken offers it once after the update, or pick "MelloUI" as the narrator style in Spoken's settings.

**Play buttons.** Read on the quest log plays Spoken's recording when it has one. You can also play a quest's voice-over from the Quest Tracker's right-click menu, and dungeon and boss stories from Dungeon Info.

**Read fix.** Read on the quest log now reads a quest even if the voice pack has no recording of it and Read Unvoiced Lines is off.

**Housekeeping.** This update adds a new file, so restart the game fully after updating (a /reload won't pick it up). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
