**2.1.66: marks on your cast bar, a darker sweep, grimoires that remember** 🔮

Everything in this one came from Glockta-code. Thank you!

**Cast Marks.** New, and off until you turn it on (Bars > Cast Bar > Bar). The end of each cast is hatched for the spell queue window plus your latency, so you can see when the next spell will queue, and a channel shows a thin mark where each tick lands: Drain Life, Mind Flay, Arcane Missiles and more. On the game's cast bar and the Personal Display's.

**Cooldowns.** Turn off Numbers In Fights and the game stops counting the global cooldown on every button in fights; the sweep shows the time instead. Sweep Darkness makes that sweep Dark or Darker.

**Demon Grimoires.** For warlocks, off until you turn it on (Quality of Life > Vendor). Summon each demon once and MelloUI remembers what it knows, then marks every grimoire at the trainer as Known or New, with a line in its tooltip. No more summoning your whole family in front of the vendor.

**Housekeeping.** This one needs a full restart of the game after updating (new files). Also fixed: your own characters could show a realm in Mail Recipients when the game named none. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
