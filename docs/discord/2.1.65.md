**2.1.65: frames that stay where you put them, a corpse you can find, mail that names itself** 📬

Most of this one came from your reports on Discord. Thank you, and keep them coming!

**Staying put.** A pet frame you moved no longer jumps under the player frame when a fight starts, and a moved totem bar keeps its place in fights too (once MelloUI has copied your Edit Mode layout, one /reload switches to the copy). Reminders detached from the portrait keep their size and place, and a druid's Druid Forms row is no longer covered by buffs anchored to the player frame.

**On the map.** Your corpse shows on the map and the minimap again; on the minimap it waits at the edge until you are close. With Questie, hold Shift over a quest icon to open its full tooltip at once. Show on a crafting writ now tells you it has no place on the map instead of doing nothing. In the Oldschool look, the Quest List tab reads clearly on the dark map, and the stray parchment is gone.

**New options.** Personal Display: Percent Position puts the health and power percents on the bars. Mail: an empty subject takes the name of the first item you attach. That one, and the configurator's tab names in your language, came from Glockta-code.

**Credits where they are due.** The Supporters page now also names everyone whose code and translations came in as pull requests on GitHub: Glockta-code, woofix, pmyszczynski, TribalInstincts and Baine. Thank you!

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating; the new Contributors list shows after a full restart of the game. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
