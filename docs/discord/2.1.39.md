**2.1.39: Questie, in MelloUI's clothes** 🧭

**Questie's brains, MelloUI's looks.** Got Questie installed? MelloUI's Quest List, map pins, objective marks and Route now read Questie's quest database for the newest quest data, and MelloUI's own fills any gaps. Quests you can't take yet (an earlier quest, a breadcrumb, a profession first) wait in the list instead of pretending they're ready.

**No more double icons.** MelloUI's quest givers, objective marks and quest areas stay on the map; Questie's icons of the same kind step aside, and its tooltips stop listing what MelloUI already shows. The map's quest tooltips got some colour too: foes red, friends green, levels in their difficulty colour. A new Icons slider in the map's title row sizes every icon, Questie's included.

**One tracker, never two.** With Questie's tracker on, MelloUI's steps aside and Questie's puts on MelloUI's look, in every skin. Each quest gets MelloUI's quest mark: click to follow it with Route, right-click for Questie's menu. Clicking a quest opens MelloUI's map, leveling guide steps dock right under it, and it stays open in dungeons. No Questie? MelloUI's own tracker now shows each quest's XP.

**Housekeeping.** New files this time: restart the game fully, a /reload isn't enough. For the Questie parts you need Questie (the CurseForge app brings QuestieDB along); without it everything works as before. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
