**2.1.21: Clicks and parchment** 📜

**Click-casting:** Clique and other click-cast addons now work on MelloUI's frames: player, target, focus, target of target, pet, party and raid, and the Personal Display. Bind your heals to a click and go.

**Chat on parchment:** the Parchment switch now works with the Game Windows chat view too, in all three skins. The chat windows get the parchment sheet, the lines dark ink, names with their class gems.

**Dark Mode:** parchment is no longer dimmed, in the chat, the Quest Tracker, tooltips, windows and pages. Dark ink on dark paper was nobody's friend.

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
