**2.1.31: Tooltips you can read in the dark** 🌙

**Dark Mode:** tooltips keep their gold titles and hints readable. With Dark Mode on, the gold in MelloUI's tooltips was dimmed along with the rest of the interface, so a city's name on the map, a dungeon's "Click: its floor plan" line or a Services button's title nearly vanished into the box. Dark Mode now darkens surfaces only, never the words: the map's cities and dungeons, the Services bar, reminders, the Quest Tracker, the bars and the rest.
