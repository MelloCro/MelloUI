**2.1.77: quest areas without Questie** 🗺️

**No Questie? Your quests still show.** MelloUI's map and minimap now show the game's own quest areas and points for every quest in your log, drawn in MelloUI's look: the gold edge and glow in your colours, with MelloUI's marks. Done quests show where to hand them in. Before, without Questie, MelloUI's map showed no quests at all.

**With Questie** nothing changes: its places come first, and the game's areas fill in only where Questie has none.
