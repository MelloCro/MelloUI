**2.1.26: Know your bags** 🎒

**Special bags:** items in a special bag (mining bag, herb bag, ammo pouch, quiver, soul bag ...) now wear that bag's colour: a coloured rim and a small mark in the corner, and so does its free slot under Empty. The foot of the bag window lists your special bags and how full they are, like "Mining Sack 4/4". Don't want it? Windows > Bags > Look > Special Bags.

**Bag kinds with several rules:** your own kinds can now hold more than one rule. Open a kind's settings and click Add Rule: an item that matches any one of its rules goes in that kind, so a single "Profession Tool" kind can take both mining picks and fishing poles. Thanks to Nixxen for both ideas!

**Track By Name:** Personal Display, Big Cooldowns tab. Type the name (or spell ID) of any buff or debuff, like Fingers of Frost, and it shows in the Tracked Spells row while it lasts on you. Handy for talent procs the class list doesn't know about. Heads up: in a fight the game only lets addons see some spells, and the ones it hides show up out of combat. Mages, please tell us whether Fingers of Frost shows during a fight! Thanks to Naz for the heads up.
