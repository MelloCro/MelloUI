**2.1.30: Buffs & Debuffs get a home** 🧭

**Buffs & Debuffs:** a page of its own in the side list. Switch MelloUI's buffs and debuffs on, pick their Anchor (by the Minimap, under your Unit Frame, or Free), and with Free press Move Them: Edit Layout opens with them already shown, no eye to click first. Your buffs and debuffs now always stand in blocks of their own, each with its own size, spacing, icons per row, grow direction and new rows. Under the player frame one row still holds both.

**Party and raid frames:** a member who walks back into range no longer stays faded until the fight ends. The fade now follows them in and out of range as they move.

**Impact Effects:** hits flash on your target's nameplate in dungeons too, where the game hides how much a hit did.

**Nameplates designer:** your plate size and the parts you placed stay after a /reload, on the plates already on screen too.

**Route with a guide (Polestar and others):** following a guide no longer gets stuck leading you to an earlier step until a /reload. If it ever sends you the wrong way again, type /route guide dump and post what the window shows.

**Configurator:** Animations, Buffs & Debuffs, Map and Supporters have icons of their own in every skin.

**Housekeeping.** New files this time: restart the game after updating, a /reload is not enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
