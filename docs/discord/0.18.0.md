**0.18.0: the "Blizzard called, they want their look back" update** 🎨

**Reskin off? Now it all fits.** Switch the painted reskin off and all of MelloUI takes the game's own look: the settings window, Fight History and Restock, every button, tab, slider and dropdown, the tooltips, the damage meter, Combat Text, Gains, Services and the swing timers. Route's World Marker becomes the game's own waypoint, the Quest Tracker gets the game's buttons and map pin, and the Quest List follows the Quest Log. Flip the switch and everything changes on the spot, no /reload.

**Fold the Quest List away.** The minimize button on its title bar tucks the list away beside the map, leaving a little Quests bookmark with your done / total count. Click it to bring the list back. It remembers how you left it.

**Swing timers, now with a "hold still!"** The shot bar has two stages: the cream reload, where you can still move, then the red aim in the last half second. Stand still and the shot goes off; step and it waits for you. The first shot after a /reload shows again, the bar no longer drops out mid-fight, and the game's own swing timers can't sneak in on top of MelloUI's.

**Also:** the Quest List's map icons stay put when you click between the map and the list, and the two windows' title bars join up neatly.

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough.
