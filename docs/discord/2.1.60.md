**2.1.60: smoother questing, and a community update** 🤝

**Smoother questing.** With Questie installed, MelloUI did a lot of needless work at every quest update: an item picked up, a kill counted, a quest finished. It read the places of every quest in your log from Questie's data again each time, which showed as a hitch. It keeps them now. Thank you to the players who reported it; if you still see hitches after updating, tell us.

Almost everything else in this one was sent in by players. Thank you, Glockta-code, woofix and TribalInstincts.

**Personal Display: Mana Regen Pause.** After a spell costs you mana, a light runs along the power bar for the five seconds your Spirit does not regenerate mana, and the bar lights up when regeneration starts again (Unit & Group Frames > Personal > Behaviour).

**Personal Display: Druid Forms.** Two slim bars under the power bar show the powers a druid cannot see right now: energy and rage in caster form, mana and rage in cat form, mana and energy in bear form. /mello personal druid shows them on any class.

**Restock.** The shopping list at a merchant has a share of its own (Shopping List Below, behind the cog of Shopping List At The Shop), so it can open sooner than the reminder comes up.

**Bags.** A weapon or armour piece you are wearing, dropped on a + slot, now goes into a free slot. Before, it went back on.

**Languages.** German: the configurator and the installer were checked line by line by a German player, and the map, the Quest List and Dungeon Info carry the game's own zone, dungeon and boss names. French: the chat, the map's boats, zeppelins, portals and tram, the damage meter's summary and the profession trainer menu are translated.

**Dungeon Info.** A boss row's drop pictures show the item's tooltip, and Shift- or Ctrl-click links the item or tries it on.

**Housekeeping.** Coming from 2.1.59, a /reload is enough; from 2.1.58 or older, restart the game fully. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
