**2.1.29: One click is enough** 🔧

**Chat:** since 2.1.24, the Minimal chat could stop taking clicks: names, item links and the Copy Chat button did nothing, and the button sometimes vanished under your mouse. A hidden chat window was sitting on top of it. That's fixed, and the Game Windows view lets clicks through the chat as before.

**Edit Layout:** in the Elements list, things the game hides on its own (the Tooltip, the loot window, durability and the like) needed a double-click to show, and Show all skipped them. One click on the eye now shows them, and Show all shows them too.

**Nameplates designer:** the elite icon (the gold dragon on elite mobs) is now a part of its own: drag it, size it or hide it. It used to hang on the raid marker, so moving the marker dragged the dragon and the enemy's buffs along with it. Changes in the designer now also show right away on the plates already on screen.

**Dark Mode:** the map's tooltips stay readable, and the boat and zeppelin lines and the quest areas on the map keep their colours instead of turning black.

**Installer:** picking Keep my layout now really keeps your own Edit Mode layout.

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
