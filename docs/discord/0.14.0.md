**The "True Colours" update** 🎨

**Pick your colours.** Six new palettes join Ember: Obsidian, Royal Azure and Fel Ember, each with a Vibrant twin. Every one brings its own painted kit, so the rails and gems change too. Find them on Home > Your setup or in Dynamic UI Modification.

**Everything casts a shadow.** A soft dark shade now hugs every window, bar, frame and the minimap, so the UI stands out from the world. Too much? One slider, or one switch per area.

**Your errand buddy.** A little round button beside your portrait nudges you when the water runs low, mail arrives, your gear wears out or a trainer has something new. Click it and the arrow shows the way. Restock even fills your bags at the shop, but only when you hit Buy.

**Questing got smarter.** The Quest Tracker puts the nearest quest first with distances, tooltips say who takes a quest back, and the flight map shows where your route flies, with a landing countdown on the arrow. Zephras Isle works properly now too.

**Also:** red errors, raid warnings and boss emotes sit on the same soft shade as zone names, the Services bar has a sixth group, Errands, and things you can't use stay red in every palette.

**Housekeeping.** The macro backup is now a switch and starts off, since the game keeps your settings. Using Mello's layout from before? Press Fit to this screen once on Home > Your setup, or type /mello layout apply. New files, so restart the game once after updating.
