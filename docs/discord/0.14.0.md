**The "True Colours" update** 🎨

**Pick your colours.** Six new palettes join Ember: Obsidian, Royal Azure and Fel Ember, each with a Vibrant twin. Every one brings its own painted kit, so the rails and gems change too. Find them on Home > Your setup or in Dynamic UI Modification.

**Everything casts a shadow.** A soft dark shade now hugs every window, bar, frame and the minimap, so the UI stands out from the world. Too much? One slider, or one switch per area.

**Gems everywhere.** Every item in your bags, bank and guild bank wears a little gem in its quality colour, so junk jumps out at a glance. Parchment tooltips put the same gem before item names, and a class gem before player names.

**Your errand buddy.** A little round button beside your portrait nudges you when the water runs low, mail arrives, your gear wears out or a trainer has something new. In inns and cities it stays up until the job is done. Click it and the arrow shows the way. Restock even fills your bags at the shop, but only when you hit Buy.

**Watch yourself grow.** A new feed beside your character shows every skill-up ("+1 Defense 57 / 80") and what you loot and buy, each line on a soft shade that fades after a few seconds.

**Out of sight, out of mind.** Fade Out Of Combat (off by default) lets your player and pet frames disappear until a fight, a target or a scratch brings them back. Thanks for the idea!

**Questing got smarter.** The Quest Tracker puts the nearest quest first with distances, tooltips say who takes a quest back, and the flight map shows where your route flies, with a landing countdown on the arrow. Zephras Isle works properly now too.

**Find it, try it.** The settings window has a search box, and everything new in this update wears a gold New tag, so you can jump straight to it. Long lists like the fonts scroll now.

**Also:** red errors, raid warnings and boss emotes sit on the same soft shade as zone names, the Services bar has a sixth group, Errands, and things you can't use stay red in every palette.

**Fixed:** opening the world map could freeze the game, first seen in gamepad mode (thanks for the report!), and spell tooltips stay readable on parchment in combat.

**Housekeeping.** The macro backup is now a switch and starts off, since the game keeps your settings. Using Mello's layout from before? Press Fit to this screen once on Home > Your setup, or type /mello layout apply. New files, so restart the game once after updating.
