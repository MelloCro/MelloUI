**0.18.5: the "is that a rare?" update** 🐺

**Rare Alert** (new widget):
- A rare or rare elite nearby pops up in your widget column the moment it's seen: on the minimap, as a nameplate, as your target or under your mouse.
- Its 3D face, a silver star for a rare (gold for a rare elite), how far it is and which way, and a bright pulse with its own sound as it appears.
- Click its face to target it; **Lead Me There** walks you to it. Dismiss it, or let it go: it leaves when the rare dies or wanders off.
- Want a look without hunting one down? Target anything and type `/mello widgets rare` (or `/mello widgets rare elite`).
- On by default, under Reminders > Widgets (its sound has a switch of its own).

**Combo Points on your target's nameplate** (rogues, and druids in Cat Form):
- Five sockets in the game's own art, sitting on top of the target's health bar: empty as soon as you target an enemy, lit as you build them, glowing when all five are full.
- The name moves up a little to make room; your debuffs, the crowd-control icon, the threat line and the cast bar all keep clear.
- Under Nameplates > Plates, with a size slider right under the switch.

**Action Bar backdrops, your way:**
- Every bar can have a backdrop now, each with its own switch (Action Bars > Backdrops): bars 1 to 8, stance, pet and possess, the micro menu, the bag bar and the experience and reputation bars.
- Whatever you place together shares one backdrop, in any shape: stacked bars, a column beside a row, the micro menu on the end of a bar. Gems on its corners, a turned joint on its inner corners.
- The experience and reputation bars stretch to fit the backdrop they're in.
- Off on a new install; the backdrops you already had stay on.

**Fix:** the target frame's combo points now sit outside the portrait ring instead of over its border.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
