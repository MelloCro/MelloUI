**2.1.46: Your profiles, on every character** 🧙

**Each character keeps its own profile.** Set up your alt without touching your main: every character now has its own profile and gets it back when it logs in. A new character starts with a copy of the one you used last, named after it. Load your main's profile on an alt and the alt gets its own copy, so nothing you change there reaches your main.

**Your changes are always saved.** No more saving and hoping: your changes go into your character's profile as you play, at every logout and /reload. Loading your own profile again takes you back to how it was last saved.

**Bars stay where you put them.** A profile now carries your Edit Mode layout too, so the action bars, bags, micro menu and everything else you moved stand in the same place on every character that uses it. Thanks, Sikotic, for the screenshots!

**The Combat Log is back in the Minimal chat.** The Combat Log tab now shows your fights as they happen (so you can finally see who got you 😈), and the game's two filters, My actions and What happened to me?, are back as tabs above it. Thanks, sh4dd0w, for tracking it down!

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
