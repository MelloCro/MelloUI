**2.1.6: Off means off** 🔌

Want MelloUI's quests, bags and chat but the game's own unit frames? Now you can have exactly that.

**Three switches that mean it:** Unit Frames, Nameplates and Action Bars. Turn one off and everything MelloUI shows there goes with it: the numbers, portraits, buffs, icons, textures and effects. That part is the game's own again, and every other feature keeps working. Thanks, OpTick!

**Where they are** (`/mello`):
- **Unit & Group Frames**, pick Player, then General: Unit Frames
- **Nameplates**, pick Enemies, then General: Nameplates
- **Bars**, pick Action Bars, then General: Action Bars (new!)

"Unit Frame Tweaks" and "Nameplate Tweaks" are the same switches with plainer names. Dark Mode and the Fader stay your own choice: if you set them up, they still apply.
