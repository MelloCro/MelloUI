**2.1.95: move your Target of Target and the target's cast bar** 🎯

**Target of Target.** It has its own spot in Edit Layout now, so you can drag it anywhere instead of having it stuck to the target frame. Until you move it, it stays where it always was.

**Target cast bar.** New option: Unit Frames > Target > Detach Cast Bar. Turn it on and your target's cast bar becomes its own thing, "Target Cast Bar" in Edit Layout, and you can put it wherever you like. It shows the spell, its icon, channels, the "can't interrupt" look and interrupts, same as the normal one. Off by default.

**Housekeeping.** This update adds a new file, so restart the game fully after updating (a /reload won't pick it up). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
