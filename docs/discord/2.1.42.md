**2.1.42: Straight from your comments** 💬

**Fog of War.** A new **Fog Of War** option (Map page) hides the places you haven't explored yet, as the game's own world map does, until you walk there. Off by default: switch it on in /mello. Thanks, whatever_69 and pkleech!

**A better whisper window.** It now stays on top of other windows, like the map and the bags, while it's open. Right-click a person to **Remove Conversation** or **Remove All Conversations**: no more endless list. It also shows all your conversations the first time it opens after you log in. Thanks, atus_dr!

**A swing ring you can see.** New **Swing Ring Width** (Thin, Medium, Thick) and **Swing Ring Colour** options (Bars › Swing Timers) for the ring that fills round your swing badge, and the part still to fill is now a dark track. The game's own swing timers no longer show idle plates in Edit Layout while the badge takes their place.

**Dark Mode fix.** An action button's gold frame for an active stance or a queued spell stays bright under Dark Mode now.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new art). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
