**The "Heads Up" update** 👀

**Widgets, not popups.** Loot rolls, ready checks, summons, resurrect offers, whispers, your corpse run, auction house news, a crafting batch: each one is now a small round widget in one tidy column, with a gold ring for its time and the buttons right on it (Need, Greed, Ready, Accept, Reply...). Voice Over moved in too, with a ring that fills as the line plays. In a fight, the chatty ones politely wait their turn.

**Threat without a meter.** In a group, a thin bar in the bottom rail of an enemy's nameplate fills up as you get close to pulling it: gold is fine, amber means ease off, red means it's coming for you. Tanks see red when a mob wanders off them.

**Bags that make sense.** Junk turns grey, so it's easy to spot, and a special bag's slots (your mining pack, herb bag or quiver) wear their bag's colour. The reminder by your portrait now warns you when your bags are nearly full, even mid-fight.

**Find your way in style.** Route's trail on the map is now a line of red dots, and the beam over your destination lands in a glowing ring instead of stopping at the gem.

**Also:** whisper windows have Add Friend, Invite, Ignore and Report buttons; the nameplate level circle sits on the diamond; reminders stay up in towns like Sentinel Hill; the Quest List's map marks work with a controller again; and settings that did the same job are merged into one (your choices carried over).

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
