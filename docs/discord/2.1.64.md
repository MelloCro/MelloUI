**2.1.64: a whole zone in one click, a sure step back, loot for Forever's own dungeons** 🗝️

Two of this one's three came from Glockta-code again. Thank you!

**Track a whole zone.** In the map's quest log, every zone's heading now has a box, and an All quests line on top has one for the whole log. One click puts all of those quests in the Quest Tracker, or takes them all off when they are all there already.

**RestedXP, one step back.** The tracker's Back arrow now goes back in RestedXP to a step you have not done yet. A done step is only shown, so RestedXP stays on its own step and Forward walks you back up to it. With nothing before it, Back is greyed instead of gone.

**Dungeon Info.** Six of Forever's own dungeons had empty loot lists. The Drowned City, Blackmaw Hold, Alcaz Prison, The Shaper's Terrace, Krol'dok Stronghold and City of Dalaran now show what drops, and more classic bosses show Forever's own loot, from DungeonMaster's lists. Drop rates players measured for Everloot show in Razorfen Kraul, Scarlet Monastery and Dalaran, with more to come as the numbers grow.
