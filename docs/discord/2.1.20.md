**2.1.20: Animations, all in one place** 🎬

**Animations:** every animation MelloUI plays now lives on one page, **Animations** (in The look): heal lights, big moments, hits on your target, bars and bags, the tracker and your route. Reduce Motion sits on top, and a **Play** button beside each one shows it once, so you can see what it is before you turn it off.

**Swing Badge:** the gold ring round your weapon now fills up as your next swing nears.

**Big Cooldowns:** square icons in the Personal Display's gold rim, with their seconds drawn and coloured just like your debuffs on the nameplates (even with the display's percent text off).

**Over The Personal Display:** the swing badge and your Big Cooldowns now share one row on the bars, the badge in the middle and the cooldowns beside it, no more tower climbing up to your feet.
