**0.19.1: the "everything matches" update** 🎨

**Ember Vibrant** (new palette):
- A brighter take on Ember: ember-black panels, vivid bronze trim, bright cream-gold text and a jewel ember red for whatever you select.
- Like Ember, it comes in **Warm iron** and **Bronze**. Pick it on Look > General, or on the installer's Look step, right under Ember.

**One look for every control:**
- Check boxes, dropdowns, search boxes, tabs, sliders, scroll bars, close buttons and the small icon buttons now match MelloUI's own settings, in the game's windows too.
- Buttons like OK, Accept and Create keep their red plate, now with clean closed ends and no diamonds on the sides.
- Reskin off? Everything keeps the game's own look.

**Settings, tidied:**
- Look > Borders now holds every border, Look > Fonts every text size and font (chat, unit frame bars, cooldown timers, the Quest Tracker, notices, combat text), and Look > Parchment every parchment switch, chat and tooltips included.
- Each element's own page still shows its setting, with a button straight to it.

**Bag bar:** a small arrow beside the backpack folds your bag slots away and brings them back. Pick up an item and they pop out on their own, so a new bag can go straight in. Don't want it? Turn off Collapse Arrow under Action Bars > Bag Bar.

**Fix:** the world map's waypoint pins are sharp again, no more blocky gem.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
