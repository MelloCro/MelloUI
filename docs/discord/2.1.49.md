**2.1.49: MelloUI with a controller** 🎮

**Play MelloUI with a gamepad.** In the game's Gamepad mode, MelloUI's windows, the configurator and its lists now work with the D-pad, with your controller's buttons shown on screen. The Game Menu opens MelloUI with Triangle (Y).

**Your controller bars in MelloUI's look.** MelloUI now shows the game's own controller bars: hold one trigger or both and that bar grows, with each button's prompt on it. Bind and Edit Action Bars work just as in the game, and every part of the bars moves on its own in Edit Layout. Your Action Bars 1-4 are copied over once, so nothing starts empty.

**The map and your bags.** MelloUI's map opens on its quest list: the left stick moves the cursor without walking you, and the D-pad switches between the map and the quests. In MelloUI's bag window A picks up, X uses or sells, and Y opens More: Bind to Action Bar, Split Stack, Destroy Item and Feed to Pet.

It's the first version, so tell us how it plays with your controller!

**Bags next to other bag addons.** With Baganator, Bagnon, ArkInventory or BetterBags installed, MelloUI's bag window steps aside, so B opens one bag window. Thanks, pmyszczynski, for the report on GitHub!

**Minimap quest marks.** Quest objectives on the minimap now look just like on MelloUI's map, with the glowing quest areas, the same icons and a dot for each spot, and they show right after you log in.

**Battle.net whispers kept.** The whisper window keeps your Battle.net friends' conversations over a logout.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
