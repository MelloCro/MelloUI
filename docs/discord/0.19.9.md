**0.19.9: keys, layouts and checkboxes** ⌨️

Bind your keys without leaving the bars, shape your bars and buffs from MelloUI, see how much of every zone you've done, and switch off the big features you don't use, right in the game's AddOns list.

**New:**
- Keybind Mode: point at an action button and press a key, a mouse button or the wheel (with Shift, Ctrl or Alt if you like). Every button shows its key big while it's on. Done keeps your changes, Escape or Cancel undoes them (Action Bars page, Edit Layout, or /mello keybind).
- Action bar layout: rows, icons, icon size and padding for each bar, with a picture of its shape (Action Bars > Bars > Layout). Saved into your Edit Mode layout; type /reload to see it.
- Buffs & Debuffs layout: icon size, spacing, per row, which way they grow and where new rows go (Unit Frames > Buffs & Debuffs > Layout).
- Quest List: every group shows how much you've done, with a bar, and the done quests fold away into their own section.
- Zone progress: a zone map shows a ring of the quests you've done there, how many are left and how many you can take now. Quest givers say how many of theirs are left.
- Your alts on quest tooltips: the Quest List and the map's quest givers name your other characters who have done a quest or have it in their log (/mello alts lists them).
- Edit Layout: a Gap box beside Snap keeps 0 to 10 px between bars you snap side by side.
- New steel-and-gold art for the close buttons, check boxes, arrows, cogs and icon buttons, and a winged portrait border for the windows and unit frames.

**Five features are addons of their own now:** Chat, Combat Text, Quest List, Route and Voice Over each get a checkbox in the game's AddOns list. Don't use one? Untick it and it isn't loaded at all. Your settings stay put, and Route's learned roads move into its own file by themselves.

**Changed:**
- The Classic and Forever logos are gone from the quest log and the Quest List.
- The quest log's difficulty diamonds sit under the tick box, clear of long quest names and the Dungeon tag.

**Fixed:**
- With Route switched off for a whole session, logging out no longer clears the roads it had learned.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating: 0.19.9 brings new addon folders, which a /reload doesn't find. Installing by hand? Copy every MelloUI_ folder into AddOns next to MelloUI.
