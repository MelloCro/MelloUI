**0.20.4: Your bags, your order** 🎒

Bags by Kind got sorted properly: the kinds you know from the game, in the order you want, and a quieter chat box.

**Bags by Kind:**
- The kinds now follow the game's item types: Hearthstone, Consumable, Quest, Weapon, Armour, Reagent, Trade Goods, Recipe, Projectile, Bag, Quiver, Key, Miscellaneous, Other, Junk and Empty. Hearthstone starts on top.
- Put them in your own order on Windows > Bags > Kinds: drag a row up or down.
- A line between sections, and groups (Equipment, Crafting) with a heading you click to fold. Add your own lines and groups, rename a group with a double-click.
- Pick up an item and every kind shows a **+** slot: drop the item there and it always goes under that kind. Drop it on its own kind's + to put it back, or Forget them all on Windows > Bags.
- Untick a kind and its items go to their own type or to Other. Your old kind switches carry over.
- Small kinds share a row with a bigger one, so Hearthstone and Consumable sit side by side.

**Chat:**
- Input Box Only While Typing (on): the input box no longer stays on screen. Press Enter and it appears, send or press Escape and it goes. Want it back? Switch it off on Chat > Chat Frame.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
