**2.1.37: Grab a corner** 📐

**Every window like the map.** The bags, Fight History and the Restock list now work like the map from 2.1.36: drag the title bar to move them, and drag the small grip in a corner to size them. For the bags, the grip sets how many columns they have (8 to 16). Right-click a grip to go back to the standard size. They keep their place and size after a reload, and Edit Layout still moves them too.

**More room in the chat.** With the Minimal Chat View, the channel list on the left can fold down to just its icons, leaving more room for your messages. Click the small handle on the list's edge, or turn on Channel List As Icons on the Chat page. Point at an icon to see its name. Thanks, NiceGuyBadGamer!

**A new Supporters page.** The people who help keep MelloUI going now have their names on three animated banners, one for each tier. The page also has MelloUI's Patreon and Discord addresses, ready to copy.
