**The World Marker, rebuilt to feel right:**
- It stands on the ground now. The game puts quest markers at sea level (166 yards under Thunder Bluff!), so ours sank with them; it now uses Route's own pin on the real spot.
- Near the place it floats above it, with gold chevrons rippling down, so it never covers the NPC you're looking for.
- Smaller the farther away, smooth at any frame rate, and the arrow round your character follows your camera zoom.
- No more jumping: pick another quest and it rises up fresh there with its beam; a nearer target and it glides over.
- A soft breath of air as it changes (Marker Sounds, on by default, under Route > Arrow & Marker).

**Smarter quests:**
- Quests where you make an item from other items (like Traditions of the Bluff: buy four things in Thunder Bluff and combine them) now lead you to each one in turn, then tell you to combine them. Every such quest, Classic and Forever.
- Forever quest items you simply buy now lead you to the vendor.
- The Quest Items widget now also pops up for an item you made yourself and use at the spot (the incense at the burner), for every such quest.

**Polish:** the glow round your widgets, and round whatever you drag in Edit Layout, is smaller and softer. Round buttons now light up round under your mouse, and square ones square.

**Fixes:**
- Route no longer sends you back to the vendors after you've made the item.
- Edit Layout no longer lets you grab an invisible frame the size of your whole screen.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
