**0.20.1: Every window, dressed properly** 🪨

A big look pass: every game window got its frame checked one by one, there's a new stone rail and a new parchment, and nothing hides under the borders anymore.

**Windows:**
- Two frames to pick from (Look > Borders > Window Border): a thin stone rail round every window, or the double rail with gem corners. Title bars as a red ribbon or a plain band with a rail line (Window Title).
- The game's own portrait and its ring stay in the top left corner, at the game's size, the backpack too.
- With the Double rail the frame now wraps the window instead of covering its edges: bag slots, money, the buttons at the bottom, search boxes and tabs stay in full view. That's the bags, merchant, mailbox, trade, guild, collections, macros, trainers, bank, guild bank, auction house, inspect, dressing room, tabard, stable, loot window and the AddOns list.
- Close buttons sit inside the title plate on every window, Options, the calendar and Edit Mode included. The empty title plates (macros, tabard designer, stable) read their titles again.
- Calendar: the month arrows sit on the title plate either side of the month. Guild bank: the money line is back, and the crest with its gold vines rides the top of the frame.
- Header bands are flat now: your palette's colour with a thin gold line, instead of the plate with gem ends.
- One look for every search box, edit box and dropdown: the Stone rail round a dark field, with a gold arrow on the dropdowns.

**New art:**
- A new parchment everywhere it shows: warm paper with burnt edges, curled corners on the big pages.
- New backgrounds: Dark gradient and Dark round gradient.
- Stone rail and Stone ring: new borders with their own dark trough for buttons, bars, unit frames, nameplates, raid frames and the minimap. Level Orb: the kit's orb or a stone badge round your level.
- A new font: MelloFont.

**Also new:**
- Loot Sparkles (Nameplates > Sparkles, off until you switch it on): quest items keep their sparkle, and golden glints mark the ore vein or herb you walk up to.
- Attach To The Player Frame: your buffs and debuffs under your player frame, like the target's under the target frame.
- Group Frames > Bars > Background: stone, parchment or a dark gradient behind the health bar, on party and raid frames alike.
- Swing Timer Border on Look > Borders.
- The configurator's What's new is up to date again.

**Fixed:**
- A shaman's weapon imbues show on your buff rows, with stones, oils and poisons.
- Nameplates and swing timers that appeared mid-fight kept the old border.
- /mello auras works again.
- The experience and reputation bars no longer show the old frame after logging in.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating: 0.20.1 brings new files (the font, the art, Loot Sparkles), which a /reload doesn't pick up. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
