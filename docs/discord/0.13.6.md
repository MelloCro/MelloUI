**The "where am I even going" update** 🧭

**Route grew a brain.** It walks you to the actual objective (the boar, not the vague yellow blob), follows roads across zones, takes the bridge like a civilised adventurer, and stops pretending you know flight paths you've never visited. Your destination now gets a red light beam you can see from orbit.

**Quests, decoded.** Every quest wears a Classic or Forever logo. "Quest giver unknown" is mostly retired: quests from mob drops get crossed swords, picked-up items get a chest, and dungeon drops send you to the entrance. There's a new Filter too, plus "Hide 5+ levels above me" so the red quests stop judging you.

**New toys** (off by default): a Quest Tracker that actually scrolls, Buffs & Debuffs rows, and Error Messages to mute "Not enough rage" for good.

**Combat:** Execute Range turns enemy bars purple below 20%, bar text keeps its format mid-fight, and opening windows in combat no longer throws ADDON BLOCKED.

**Also:** whispers can pop out into their own little windows, windows fade in (or don't, with Reduce Motion), parchment is optional, and profiles can be shared with a single string.
