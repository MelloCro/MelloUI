**0.20.0: Group Frames and Frame Effects** 💚

MelloUI's own party and raid frames are here, built for healers: put your HoTs, shields and debuffs where you want them, colour a frame when a HoT is on it, and watch your heals fly from your cast bar to whoever you're healing.

**Group Frames (new):**
- MelloUI's own party and raid frames, in place of the game's. Size, spacing, which way the party grows, its order (role, group or name), how the raid is grouped (group, role or class). Move them in Edit Layout. Out of range members fade.
- Bars your way: health in class colour, your own colour or by health (three colours you pick), with a background and missing health colour of their own. Power bars for healers or everyone. Incoming heals, shields and the debuff glow included.
- Texts: the name (white, class colour or yours, shortened if you like), health as a percent, current or missing, and Dead or Offline.
- Every colour of your own comes from a colour wheel.

**The Designer (Group Frames > Designer):**
- Your own aura indicators: a buff by its spell (yours or anyone's, every rank) or a debuff by its kind (dispellable by you, Magic, Curse, Disease, Poison, boss, crowd control).
- Show each one as an icon with a sweep, a draining icon, a draining square, a bar along an edge, or the whole health bar in your colour while it's up (Rejuvenation turning the bar green, say).
- Nine spots, an offset, a size, a colour, and the time and stacks as text, counting down as a plain number. They work in a fight.
- Your frame big on the page: drag an indicator to move it, scroll to size it. Move, size or hide the name, health text, role icon, raid mark and ready check too.
- Every class starts with a set of its own, and each spec keeps its own list.
- Show the fake raid on screen to see it all in place.

**Prefer the classic portrait party frames?** In A Party (Group Frames > Frames) switches a party to the normal party frames. They get indicators and a Designer of their own (buffs, name, role, leader icon and ready check), sharp role badges, and you show as one of them while solo. They move in Edit Layout now, just like the raid frames.

**Frame Effects (new):**
- Heal Flight: your heal gathers on your cast bar and flies to their frame. HoTs sink in, shields wrap the frame, protection puts a crest on it, a heal to full raises a column of light, a resurrection lights the fallen one. Group heals reach everyone; Chain Heal hops.
- Four looks: Holy, Nature, Water or Quiet. By Class picks one for you. HoT ticks pulse on the frame, and raids get a calmer landing so the frames stay readable.
- Big moments show on your frame and your group's frames: roars and taunts, Shield Wall and Barkskin, Last Stand's red heartbeat, Divine Shield and Ice Block, Evasion and more.
- Portrait frames (your player frame, the party frames) get the effect on the ring and on the bars. It sits under the portrait's ring and level badge, and what lands on you shows on your player frame too.
- Try it all with /mellofx (every heal kind), /mellofx heal, /mellofx fx, and /mellofx stop.

**Also new:**
- Hide PvP Badge (on by default): no more diamond honour badge beside your portrait while you're flagged.

**Fixed:**
- Player Portraits: your own frame no longer slips back to the game's portrait after a /reload or while you're flagged for PvP.
- /mellobug now catches blocked actions from MelloUI's own feature addons too.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating: 0.20.0 brings two new addon folders (MelloUI Group Frames, MelloUI Frame Effects), which a /reload doesn't find. Installing by hand? Copy every MelloUI_ folder into AddOns next to MelloUI. Want the game's own party and raid frames back? Switch Group Frames off on its page, or untick MelloUI Group Frames in the AddOns list.
