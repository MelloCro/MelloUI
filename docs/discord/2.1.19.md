**2.1.19: Aspects, plates and flags** 🏹

**Aspects and stances:** the hunter's Aspects, the warrior's stances and the druid's forms now open on MelloUI's action bars, and the spell you pick casts, in a fight too.

**Nameplates:** only your own debuffs on enemy plates (Only My Debuffs, on by default), no clock sweep on them, and their numbers turn red under 5 seconds. Move the combo points with two offsets, and size the swing badge from 50 to 200 %. The nameplate preview now shows the swing badge and the name where the plates put them.

**Personal Display:** a new Over The Personal Display switch puts the swing badge above the display.

**Battlegrounds:** the map now shows you, your group and the flags.

**Action bars:** after moving MelloUI's pet bar, the game's hidden pet bar no longer blocks clicks where it used to be. Thanks to Baine for the fix!

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
