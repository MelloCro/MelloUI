**2.1.25: Hands off my potions** 🎒

**Bags:** clicking items in MelloUI's bag window could get blocked with "MelloUI took an action reserved for the Blizzard UI", for a whole session. We finally caught it: when the bag window made item buttons during a fight (opening your bags for the first time in combat, or loot landing in an empty slot mid-fight), the game marked those buttons, and blocked clicks on them from then on. The bag window now makes all its buttons before a fight, never during one. Huge thanks to Nixxen for the detailed /mellobug reports! If you still see that message after updating, please send us a /mellobug report.
