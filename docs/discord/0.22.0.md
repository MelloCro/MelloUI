**0.22.0: Tidy up** 🧹

Your bags, your minimap and your split stacks all got a little neater, mostly straight from your requests.

**New:**
- **Reagent Bag tab:** with a Reagent Bag on, the bag window gets two tabs on its left side, Backpack and Reagent Bag. Move items between them like anywhere in your bags, or drop an item on a tab to put it in that bag's first free slot (hold it over a tab and the tab opens). The Reagent Bag's button opens the window on its tab.
- **AddOns group:** your addons' minimap buttons (Questie and the others) leave the minimap's edge and move into one more group in the Services row, AddOns. Click it for the list; a click there is the addon's own, its tooltip too. Off on Minimap > Services Bar.
- **Split Stack:** scroll the mouse wheel over the split box to change the amount (Shift: ten at a time), and a ½ button halves the stack (191 makes 95). The amount sits on a dark field now too.

**Installer:**
- Mello's layout fits screens at many more UI scales, where it said "too small" before.
- Edit Mode already full of layouts? Install without the layout, or delete an older MelloUI one (it asks first). No more being stuck.

**Fixed:**
- Group Frames: the random yellow box beside your party or raid frames is gone.
- Heal Effects: spamming a heal no longer leaves the light looping on your cast bar.
- Minimap: on the square map, the day / night moon sits in the map's top right corner instead of on the zone name.

**Housekeeping.** Nothing to restart beyond the usual update. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
