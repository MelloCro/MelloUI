**2.1.18: Stock up** 🧺

**Restock List:** it's now a catalogue. Pick a kind on the left (drink, food, ammo, potions, elixirs, scrolls, poisons, reagents), tick each item you want to keep and set how many. It shows the items for your level, items have their tooltips, and a search finds anything else.

**Hunters:** keep pet food by diet: meat, fish, bread, cheese, fruit or fungus. It counts every food of that diet in your bags and buys the best one a shop has.

**Search boxes:** text in MelloUI's search and input boxes starts at the left again.

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
