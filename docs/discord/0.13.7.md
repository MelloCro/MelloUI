**The "First Impressions" update** ✨

**Hello, installer.** Your first login now greets you with a setup: Full experience, No reskin, Reskin only, or Fresh start, which walks you through it step by step (round or square minimap, which features you want). It fits Mello's layout to your screen, and if you don't like the result, it goes back on its own after 15 seconds.

**A new home for your settings.** The configurator got rebuilt: groups down the left, the emblem on top, and a Home page with your setup, What's new and a quick tour.

**The minimap grew up.** It's bigger and as wide as the Quest Tracker, with the Services buttons grouped under a stone rail and your buffs lined up beside it. The tracker always stays on top.

**Easier on the eyes.** Centre-screen messages, zone names, map markers, the Route arrow and nameplate names sit on a soft dark shade instead of a harsh outline. Route now tells you how long the trip will take.

**Two folders, and lighter.** Route's road map moved into `MelloUI_Companion`: copy both folders into AddOns and restart the game once (the CurseForge app does it for you). The artwork dropped from 96 MB to about 30 MB.

**Also:** every window gets the look, your chat background opacity travels with your profile, Dark Mode stays your own call, Voice Over can stay quiet on lines nobody recorded, and `/melloperf` shows what MelloUI costs you.
