A small one, for a player who asked on CurseForge. Thank you for telling us!

**Press and Hold Casting works on MelloUI's action bars.** With the game's option on (Options > Combat), hold a key and the spell keeps casting until you let go, on Action Bar 1 and Bars 2 to 8 alike. The buttons still light up while you hold them. With the option off, nothing changes.
