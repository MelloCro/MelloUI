**2.1.63: a marker that stands where it should, guides your way, steady action bars** 🧭

**World Marker on the right spot.** On a quest, the marker now stands on the game's own point for it, at its real height, so it no longer floats in the air when you look down from a hill. Off screen, its arrow rides an oval round the middle of the screen in the place's exact direction, as the game's own focus marker does. MelloUI also switches on the game's in-game navigation while the World Marker is on, so the game's marker fills in wherever ours steps aside. If a place ever puts the marker under the ground, `/route gamepoint` switches back to MelloUI's own pin.

**Leveling guides.** When a guide's step is about a quest already in your log, MelloUI focuses that quest instead of setting a map pin, so the marker stands right on it. Each guide's card under Addons > Installed now has Guide steps in: MelloUI's Quest Tracker (the default), the guide's own window, or both side by side. With Polestar, the tracker also shows who or where each step is, and Polestar's note under its goals. Parts of this came from pmyszczynski's pull request, thank you.

**Action bars.** The range colour no longer sticks after a vehicle, possession or form bar. Cooldown numbers no longer vanish in fights where the game hides cooldown times from addons: the game's own numbers count them then.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
