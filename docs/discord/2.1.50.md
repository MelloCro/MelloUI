**2.1.50: a smooth map, smarter bags, and a horseshoe** 🐎

**A map that keeps up.** Carrying a pile of quests no longer makes the world map stutter on open or go grey at the middle zooms, however many you take on. The quest areas look exactly as before. Thanks, Nixxen!

**Shopping and tidying.** Restock's list at a merchant has a tick box on every row: untick an item to skip it at every shop. Food, drink and ammo below the best you carry aren't offered any more. Discard gets a **Consumables Too** switch, a right-click on its button always keeps that item, and with AHledger installed it knows what sells for more at the auction house. Nixxen again!

**Little helpers.** Ctrl+Click an item on the Loot Rolls card or in Dungeon Info to try it on. The Crafting card now counts down the whole stack. The Quest Tracker's new **Nearest Turn In** check mark follows the nearest quest that's ready to hand in, then the next one (thanks, Tereith). Hunters get a **Stable Master** button in Services, with a horseshoe of its own (thanks, TribalInstincts).

**Minimap and chat.** The minimap now wears the big map's look for RareScanner and flight masters, and no longer doubles up Questie's quest icons. A new **Fake Link Warning** flags chat links dressed up as something else, like a Community invite pretending to be an item.

**Plus de français.** More of the bag window, the profiles, Restock, Discard and Services now speaks French. Merci, woofix!

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
