**1.0: MelloUI, all grown up** 🗺️

This is the big one. Everything I promised in "Where MelloUI is heading" is in, plus a world map of its own.

**New:**
- **A world map of MelloUI's own:** one seamless map of the whole world. The mouse wheel zooms from Azeroth down to a village, a drag looks around, and up close it shows the minimap's own ground and the caves under the hills. You, your party, Route's way, flight masters, boats, dungeons, quest givers and your quests' objectives are all on it. Press **M**.
- **Your quest log beside the map (L):** your quests with their objectives, story and rewards, the Quest List as a second tab, search, a pin to share in chat, and a gear to switch each layer. Questie, HandyNotes and GatherMate2 pins show up on it too.
- **One clean look:** Gilded Slate colours, the Fredoka font and a new logo across MelloUI's own windows, bars and frames, with a new icon set that stays sharp at every size.
- **MelloUI Oldschool:** the painted stone-and-parchment look now lives in its own addon, a separate download. Install it beside MelloUI and everything painted is back, your settings included.
- **Smarter navigation:** Route takes the shortcuts the ground allows, finds its way out of rough hills, keeps to the streets and lifts in the capitals, and never spins you round mid-walk.
- **RestedXP Compatibility:** MelloUI follows RestedXP Guides' steps: the map pin, Route, the World Marker and the guide's current steps at the top of the Quest Tracker.
- **A friendlier settings window:** Unit & Group Frames, Nameplates, Bars and Minimap are designers now. Click a part on the preview and drag names and values where you want them.
- **Quest Tracker in the chat's look,** with the game's own clicks, round marks and a difficulty gem per quest.
- **A fresh Full experience:** the installer sets up the new layout and settings, fitted to your screen, from 1080p up to ultrawide.

**Housekeeping.** Updating by hand? Delete your old **MelloUI** folder and every **MelloUI_** folder in `Interface\AddOns` first, then unzip the new ones (your settings are kept, they live in WTF). Still have the old **MelloUI_VoiceOverData** voice pack? Delete it too. The CurseForge app does all of this for you. New files: restart the game after updating, not just /reload. Want the painted look? Grab **MelloUI Oldschool** from CurseForge too. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
