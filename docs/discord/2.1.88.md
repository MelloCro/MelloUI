**2.1.88: class coloured names on nameplates** 🎨

**Class Coloured Player Names.** Players' names on their nameplates can now be in their class colour. There are two switches, both off by default: one for enemy players (Nameplates > Enemies, click the name) and one for friendly players (Nameplates > Friendly players, click the name). NPCs keep the game's colours.

**Good to know.** If you picked your own class colours on Look > Colours, the names use those. It also works with Show Names As, so a shortened name gets the class colour too.
