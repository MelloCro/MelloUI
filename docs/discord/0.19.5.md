**0.19.5: the quest map update** 🗺️

**New:**
- Objective marks: the world map shows where each open objective of your quests is done, a soft area with its outline, and a dot for every spot when you zoom in. Hover for the mob, its level and the drop chance; click to set a waypoint. The same marks show on the minimap for what's near you, with an arrow on its edge for the nearest ones just past it.
- Forever's own quests learn their objective spots as you play: each kill or loot that counts is remembered for all your characters.
- Hide a quest you don't want: Alt-click its giver's mark on the map or the quest in the list. Show hidden quests in the list's gear menu brings one back.
- Hide Grey Quests (Quest List > Map) keeps the map free of quests far below your level.
- Trail Look (Route > Look): Red Beads, Gilded Line or Waymarks. The destination is now a flag in a gold ring, on the World Marker too.
- New /mellobug: if the game ever says MelloUI was blocked, it shows a report ready to copy and send us.

**Changed:**
- The map only shows quests you can actually pick up: no more other classes' quests, finished chain steps or quests waiting on a breadcrumb.
- The game's own blue quest areas step aside while the objective marks are on, and come back when you turn them off.

**Voice pack:** still the one from 0.18.3. Already have it? Nothing to download.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game after updating: the learned spots use a new save file and the map dots are new art, which a /reload doesn't load.
