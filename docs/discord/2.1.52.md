**2.1.52: quest moments, and Questie takes over the quest desk** 📜

**Quest Moments.** The game's yellow quest lines in the middle of the screen become moments. An item you pick up counts up in a gilded ring and flies to its line in your tracker, a finished objective closes its ring with a flare and a tick, and a finished quest lands a gilded crest with its title. Each has its switch on the Animations page.

**Quests come from Questie now.** MelloUI no longer ships quest data of its own. With Questie installed, the map, the minimap, the Quest List and Route to a quest all run on Questie's data, in MelloUI's look. Without Questie no quests are shown, and the map and the Quest List offer a **Get Questie** button instead. Questie's switch on the Addons page is now named **MelloUI's look**: off, you get Questie's own icons and tracker. Gone with the old data: the Attunements view and Route's "First: loot" line.

**Questie, settled in.** Questie 12.9's icons show on MelloUI's map again, and its tracker hangs under MelloUI's minimap wherever you haven't moved it.

**Tidied up.** In the Configurator, labels no longer run under their sliders and the side list lines up. On the nameplates, the CC icon, Quest icon, Threat line and Combo points have one switch each. A brand-new character now gets the installer after its intro cinematic, instead of never.

**From the community.** MaxDPS lights up MelloUI's action buttons, Polestar's Game Menu button gets a row of its own, and the map remembers what you explored while it was closed (thanks, pmyszczynski). Restock's **Fill All Ammo Bags** keeps your quivers full, and the backpack stays put when the bag bar folds (thanks, TribalInstincts). Et encore plus de français: the map, Dungeon Info, Services, Restock and Discard (merci, woofix).

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
