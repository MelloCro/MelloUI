**0.18.1: the "take the tram, not the walls" update** 🚋

**Route finds its way through the capitals.** Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Orgrimmar, Thunder Bluff and Undercity: Route now knows their streets. It walks you in through the gates and down the right street instead of hugging the city walls (or cutting straight through them).

**Portal and tram.** Route now uses the portal between Rut'theran Village and Darnassus and the Deeprun Tram between Stormwind and Ironforge, both ways, and counts the ride in your arrival time. They show on the world map next to the boats and zeppelins.

**The arrow moved in.** The navigation arrow now lives in the widget column, turning in its round button with the place and the distance beside it. The separate arrow on the screen is gone. And Stop really means stop now: a tracked quest's route stays off until you pick something new.

**The Quest List, now in the quest log.** No more extra window beside the map. A button on the quest log's top row flips between the Quest Log and the Quest List, and the map remembers which one you left open.

**Unit frames.** Long names don't fight the plate behind them any more, because the plate is gone: a soft shade sits behind every name instead, like on the nameplates. The level sits on a dark disc too, nice and readable.

**Also:** the Friends button joined the other chat buttons in the column on the left, Chinese and other alphabets no longer turn into little boxes with a MelloUI font, and dead targets stop glowing purple from Execute Range.

**Housekeeping.** New files, so restart the game once after updating (a /reload won't do).
