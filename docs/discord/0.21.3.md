**0.21.3: Little moments** ✨

The small things now get a bit of ceremony, and your UI arrives fully dressed.

**New animations (each on, each switchable):**
- **The Wisp:** pick a destination and a small light takes off from you, rises, hangs a beat and darts to the place, the same flight every time. The World Marker itself is cleaner and smaller too.
- **Arrival Light:** arriving at a pin, a quest giver, a turn-in or into an objective area puts the place out in a ring of light.
- **Route Draws In:** a new route grows from you to the flag on the world map and the minimap.
- **Progress Motion:** quest counts roll over to their new number, a finished objective gets a tick.
- **Loot Flight:** loot flies into your bags, and the rarer it is, the bigger the show. A legendary rises in a pillar of light. Quest money flies in as coins.
- **Level Up:** your level number turns over with a ring of light.
- Try them anytime: `/mello route draw`, `arrive` or `wisp`, `/mello tracker progress`, `/mello bags loot`, `/mello player level`.

**Also new:**
- Party members show their level in a circle at their portrait.
- After a login or /reload your UI stays out of sight until it's fully dressed, then fades in at once. No more game look first and MelloUI arriving piece by piece. Route is ready about three times sooner.
- Tooltip: Hide Issue Report Line (on) takes the beta's "Press F6 to submit an issue" line out of your tooltips.
- Quest List: titles and levels straight from the game, and 75 more of Forever's quests.
- The latest patch's data: new cooldowns, heals, stones and oils, plus many more roads and places for Route.

**Fixed:**
- The Personal Display faded by the Fader put an invisible layer over the screen that ate your right-click camera. Gone.
- Route no longer sends you cross-country from places off the main roads.

**Housekeeping.** Nothing to restart beyond the usual update. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
