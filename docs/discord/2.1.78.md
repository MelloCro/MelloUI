**2.1.78: the minimap without Questie, and controller fixes** 🎮

Thank you woofix for the French translations!

**Minimap without Questie.** With Questie off, the minimap showed the game's own blue quest circles next to MelloUI's marks. While MelloUI marks your quests on the minimap and Hide The Game's Quest Areas is on (Quest List > Map), those circles are hidden now, as they already were on the map. Turn that switch or MelloUI's minimap marks off and they come back.

**The game's world map.** With MelloUI's map turned off, the game's own world map now shows MelloUI's quest areas without Questie too.

**French.** Loot rolls, the layout picker, the Addons page, auras, nameplates, Group Frames, Fight History, bag rules, key bindings and many more lines are now in French.

**Fixes.**
- When an addon showed or hid its minimap button during a fight, the game blocked MelloUI from moving the Services bar and the minimap's frame. They now settle once the fight is over.
- Controller: with the Minimal Chat View on, Background Opacity no longer touches the game's hidden chat windows. Those changes could make the game block actions after you opened the chat with a controller. If you still see blocked actions with a controller, please send your /mellobug report: it now names more of what went wrong.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
