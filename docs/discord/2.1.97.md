**2.1.97: coordinates in chat, and Uldaman's side door** 🧭

**Type coordinates.** /way 45.2 63.8 shows you the way to that spot on your current zone's map. Add a zone name for somewhere else: /way Westfall 45.2 63.8. Got TomTom? Then /way stays TomTom's (Route already follows its arrow), and /route 45.2 63.8 does the same thing.

**Click coordinates.** When someone posts coordinates in chat, like 45.2 63.8 or 45, 63, they turn into a gold link. Click it and Route shows you the way. Don't want that? Route > Clickable Coordinates In Chat turns it off.

**Every dungeon entrance on the map.** Dungeons with more than one entrance only showed one of them on MelloUI's map. Now you'll see them all, like Uldaman's side entrance to the Hall of the Keepers, plus the other doors of Maraudon, Dire Maul and Gnomeregan.
