**0.21.0: The Personal Display** 🛡️

Your health, power and cast bar now sit right under your character, Legion style, and every shield on you gets a column of its own. It's off by default: switch it on under Personal Display.

**Personal Display (new):**
- Health, power and cast bar under your feet, in the unit frames' bracket. The cast bar only shows while you cast. Width, Height and Edit Layout put it where you want it.
- Follow Camera Zoom keeps it under your feet as you zoom, never over your character. It even hops when you jump.
- The shield column: every shield on you (spells, talents, items, the ones against one kind of damage too), each in its school's colour with its icon. It fills when a shield goes up, drains as it takes hits, turns amber when low and shatters when the last one breaks.
- Big Cooldowns: Shield Wall, Evasion, Ice Block, Recklessness, a trinket's and friends show as icons above your health bar with their seconds, the most important first. Big Cooldown Size makes them as big as you like.
- Your swing timer rides on the bars' rails: two sparks run out to the ends as your next swing comes due. Bows, guns and wands show the reload and the aim on the seam.
- With Heal Effects on, your heals' light can gather at your character and fly from there to your party or raid frames.
- Its border follows Look > Borders, and the Fader can fade it out of combat.

**Settings:** shared options now have a link where you'd look for them: Window Border on the Windows page, Bar Texture on Group Frames, Hide The Game's Cast Bar on Cast Bars, and more.

**Fixes:**
- No more crash after a long session with the Single rail round nameplates.
- Fader: the Quest Tracker on On Mouseover comes back when you point at it, the player frame stays faded while you regenerate, and no error at login with your buffs faded.
- `/mello widgets column` lists the widget column again.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully once after updating (a new addon folder and new bar art); a /reload won't pick them up. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
