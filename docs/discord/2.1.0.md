**2.1.0: Maps all the way down** 🗺️

Now you can see where you're going even underground, every frame on your screen is MelloUI's own, and shields finally show.

**Dungeon maps:** inside a dungeon or raid, the map opens on its own floor plan, with Dungeon Info's bosses and loot beside it. The classic ones wear Blizzard's own drawn maps. Forever's own (City of Dalaran, Ruins of Lordaeron, Hall of Thanes, Excavation Site) are Santiago Reyes' "Atlas de Azeroth: Forever" maps, used with his kind permission. Thank you, Santiago!

**The whole world:** zoomed out, the capitals wear gold seals with their own emblems, boats and zeppelins leave gold trails from dock to dock, and Zephras Isle is on the map. Your quest log, the map's layers and Dungeon Info sit in a panel beside the map, never over it.

**Your frames, everywhere:** player, target, focus, pet, action bars 1-8, the stance and pet bars, the experience and bag bars: MelloUI's own in every skin, and the game's party frames stop photobombing yours. Every health bar shows shields, overshields too, and party members out of range fade.

**Edit Layout:** a Layout list to switch, copy and share Edit Mode layouts, Revert and Default place, and frames that snap together as in Edit Mode. Profiles remember where everything stands, and one made on another screen offers Fit to this screen. 32:9 folks: your corners are real corners now.

**Also:** Route follows Polestar's guide steps, and a new Supporters page under Profiles says thank you to everyone keeping MelloUI going.

**Housekeeping.** New files this time: restart the game fully after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
