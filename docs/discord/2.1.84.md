**2.1.84: the configurator tidied up, and Mail Recipients redone** ✉️

**Configurator.** On Unit & Group Frames, Nameplates, Bars and Minimap a frame's tab now shows all its settings in one scroll: the frame's own first, then each part under its own heading. Click a part on the preview and the page jumps to it. Whether a part shows is a switch in the Parts list beside the preview, and only there.

**Where things went.** A feature's on/off switch is a bar on top of its tab. The side list fits every page without scrolling: Home, Edit Layout, Profiles, Addons and Supporters are the icon buttons on top. Quality of Life has four tabs in place of twelve, and Profiles tells you what a pasted string holds before you import it.

**Heads up.** Hide PvP Badge, Hide Status Glow and Hide Combat Flash are Show switches in the Parts list now, so on means shown. Your settings are kept.

**Mail Recipients.** The list beside Send Mail is new: class pictures, levels, a green dot on a friend who is playing. Make your own groups, see your characters' professions (the Professions view lists who has which), type in To to search the list, your guild and your friends, and find the last five names you wrote to under Recent. Deleted characters no longer stay in the list. To see an alt's professions, log in on it once.

**Nameplates.** With Hide At Full Health on, the mouseover highlight no longer shows round a hidden bar. Thank you Wolvegrimm for the report!

**Controller.** Still getting "MelloUI has been blocked"? Please send your /mellobug report again: it notes more of what happened now.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
