**2.1.24: Move it, copy it, track it** 🛠️

**Nameplate Designer: ready to test!** Open /mello → Nameplates → Enemies. The preview now shows every part of the enemy plate at once, and you can drag any of them wherever you like: the name, cast bar, buffs, your debuffs, level badge, raid marker, CC and quest icons, combo points, threat line and swing badge. Scroll to resize, right-click to hide a part or put it back, and give your plates their own width and height with **Own Plate Size**. Parts stacked on the same spot get a red outline. It's brand new, so please give it a spin and report any issues you run into here on Discord. Thanks to Patch for the push!

**Copy Chat:** hover the chat and a small copy button appears in its top right corner. One click opens the tab's lines, each with its time, ready to copy. Thanks S1lver!

**Tracked Spells:** the Personal Display can now show spells you pick, like Weakened Soul, Power Word: Shield or Renew, in a small row under the bars while they last on you. Tick them on the Personal Display's Cooldowns tab. Thanks S1lver!

**Housekeeping.** New files this time: restart the game fully after updating (a /reload is not enough). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
