**2.1.28: Pick your buffs** 🧪 *Test build*

This one is a test build: the new option below hasn't been through a full round of play yet. Please try it and tell us in #bug-reports if anything looks off.

**Which Buffs:** you can now choose which of your buffs you get reminded about. Under Quality of Life > Widgets, below Your Buffs and Your Buffs On The Group, each of your class's buffs has its own switch for you and, if it goes on others, one for the group. Turn Inner Fire off and you're never asked for it again; turn Fortitude off for the group only and you're still reminded to keep it on yourself. Everything starts on, so nothing changes until you switch something off. Thanks to Emghey for the idea!
