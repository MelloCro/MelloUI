**1.2.0: Know before you go** 🗝️

Every dungeon's bosses and loot beside the map, and MelloUI dressed in gold.

**New:**
- **Dungeon Info:** every dungeon and raid next to MelloUI's map, its bosses in order and what each one drops. Open it with the door button over the map, a click on a dungeon's door, or `/dungeoninfo`.
- **Star your loot:** star the items you're after. When one drops, its loot roll shines gold and lights Need, so you can't miss it.
- **Pick Up Quests:** one click and Route walks you to every quest of that dungeon in the fastest order, then to its door.
- **Gold everywhere:** MelloUI's windows, the widget column and the Personal Display wear a gold frame, with new class portraits in the modern look.
- **Swing Badge:** your swing timer sits on your target's nameplate and flashes gold on crits.
- **More motion:** Level Up, Big Cooldowns, combo points, quest progress and reputation gains all got something to show off. Icons stay sharp at every size.
- **Map:** the gaps between zones are painted in, zooming is smooth, and quest areas are soft gold shapes that follow the spawns.
- **Route:** follows more levelling guides, and its roads take about 20 MB less memory.

**Fixed:** Silithus and six other zones are no longer cut out of the map.

**Housekeeping.** New files this time: restart the game after updating, not just /reload. Updating by hand? Unzip every folder from the zip; **MelloUI_DungeonInfo** is new among them. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
