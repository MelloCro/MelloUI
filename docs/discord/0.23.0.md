**0.23.0: A chat of its own** 💬

The chat got a makeover. It's optional, so nothing changes until you switch it on: **/mello → Chat → Chat Frame → Look → Chat View: Minimal**.

**New:**
- **Minimal chat:** one clean panel. Your chat windows sit as round tabs on the left with unread counts, each message under its sender's name, channel and time, and a row of round buttons below to talk in Say, Party, Raid, Guild and the rest.
- **Whisper window:** every conversation in one window that slides up out of the chat. Theirs on the left, yours on the right, like a messenger, with Add Friend, Invite, Ignore and Report up top. A new whisper puts a count on the Whispers tab.
- **Tabs your way:** right-click a tab for the game's own menu (rename, new window, settings), channels you /join get a tab of their own, and you can drag tabs into any order you like.
- **Smooth scrolling** in the chat and the whisper window, and parchment if your chat wears it, class gems and all.
- **Sharp round borders:** the Services buttons and the Round Border styles no longer show a dotted, cut-off ring at small sizes.

**Housekeeping.** New files this time: restart the game after updating, not just /reload. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
