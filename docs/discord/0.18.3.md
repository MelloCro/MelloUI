**0.18.3: the "they found their voice (again)" update** 🎙️

**⚠️ NEW VOICE PACK: download it again!**
Every voice was rated by ear and re-recorded at a better pace, the Forever Narrator now reads every quest's objectives, and the newest quests are voiced too.
👉 [**Voice Pack part 1**](https://github.com/MelloCro/MelloUI/releases/download/v0.18.3/MelloUI_VoicePack_Part1.zip) + [**Voice Pack part 2**](https://github.com/MelloCro/MelloUI/releases/download/v0.18.3/MelloUI_VoicePack_Part2.zip) (2.5 GB, you need both)
1. Delete your old `MelloUI_VoicePack` folder in `AddOns`.
2. Unzip both parts into `AddOns`: they make one `MelloUI_VoicePack` folder.
3. Restart the game and tick MelloUI Voice Pack in the addon list.
Your old pack keeps working until you swap.

**Two new sliders:**
- Chat: Border Opacity, right next to the background one.
- Tooltip: Fade Delay, how long a creature's tooltip stays after your cursor leaves it (0.1 to 1 second).

**Quest List:** 96 new Forever quests, and about 200 quests that showed for both factions now only show for their own.

**Fixes:**
- The painted nameplates no longer flicker as they move: the bar, its border and the level ring move as one, and the bar's colour stays inside the border.
- The Friends button beside the chat no longer shows a second ring under its own.

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough for MelloUI itself.
