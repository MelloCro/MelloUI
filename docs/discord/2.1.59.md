**2.1.59: quest animations you can read, and a calmer Oldschool** 📜

**Quest Moments.** The item picked up animation was gone before you could look at it. Its icon and count now stay two seconds before they fly to your tracker, in every look, and the ring's counted arcs are gold again.

**Oldschool.** The three quest animations wear your Frame Set: the icon in the set's ring, the count on a small plate, a finished objective or quest on a plaque of the set's frame. Progress bars wear the set's own bar frame in place of the bracket with diamond caps: reputation, skills, professions, experience, tooltips, the damage meter. List rows are calm lines on a dark ground in place of thick gold bars, with a gold marker on the picked row. And Reputation no longer opens with empty rows right after you log in.

**Small lights.** In Oldschool and Default, the little lights of map pins, quest marks, reminders, combo points, the quest tracker and Combat Text are drawn in the look's own art.

**Mail Recipients.** New, from the community (thank you, Glockta-code): a button beside the mailbox's To field lists every other character of your account and your Battle.net friends' characters. Click one and the name is filled in.

**Housekeeping.** Restart the game fully after updating (new files). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
