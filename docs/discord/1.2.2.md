**1.2.2: Loot, finally** 💎

Dungeon Info learns Forever's loot, and every dungeon wears its own picture at last.

**New:**
- **Trash loot:** every dungeon and raid has a Trash section: the blue and purple drops of its other creatures, who drops each, and the chance.
- **Forever's loot:** the bosses of the Hall of Thanes, the Ruins of Lordaeron and the Excavation Site list their loot, and the classic dungeons Forever changed show Forever's drops, from DungeonMaster's lists by MalkDev.
- **A new look:** every dungeon shows its own picture, cut to fit and never stretched: on its row in the list, behind its name, and on its bosses' pages.
- **Help fill the gaps:** Dungeon Info counts what falls in front of you, so drops get real chances. Type `/dungeoninfo export` and post the text in our Discord: it goes into the next update for everyone.
- **Got DungeonJournal?** Its loot lists show in Dungeon Info too.

**Housekeeping.** No new files this time: a /reload after updating is enough. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3, nothing to download.
