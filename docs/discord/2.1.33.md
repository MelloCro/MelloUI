**2.1.33: Oldschool stays painted** 🎨

**Oldschool fixed.** After an update the painted look could switch off, with a note that MelloUI Oldschool is from another version. CurseForge checks MelloUI and MelloUI_Oldschool separately, so the app often brings one before the other. From now on, any Oldschool from the same 2.1 series works with MelloUI, so the look stays on while CurseForge catches up.

**Minimap following your cursor?** We're on it. If it happens to you, tell us whether it starts after clicking or dragging the minimap, and whether a /reload stops it.

**Housekeeping.** Coming from 2.1.32, a /reload is enough; from 2.1.31 or older, restart the game fully (2.1.32 brought a new file). Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
