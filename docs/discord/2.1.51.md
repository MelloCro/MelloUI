**2.1.51: more guides, two screens, and a tidier minimap** 🧭

**More leveling guides.** The Addons page now lists Follow The Arrow, Azeroth Pilot Reloaded, Guidelime and FlowUp next to RestedXP, Zygor, Polestar and TomTom. Each guide's card gets a **Show its own arrow too** switch, for anyone who likes that guide's arrow on screen next to the World Marker.

**Two screens?** With Offhand spreading your UI over two monitors, the World Marker's arrow and the Level Up card's wisp now stay on your character's screen instead of floating over the gap between them. Thanks, Shumi!

**Shopping and tidying, part two.** Discard gets a **Trade Goods Too** switch for cheap crafting materials like light leather and boar meat, and its tooltip now says what a left-click does. Restock's way to a shop skips shops that only sell worse than what you carry, and tells you when one has nothing better. Nixxen strikes again!

**A calmer minimap.** With Questie, the minimap now looks just as it does without it: no second ! or ? on quest givers, and Questie's objective swords no longer pop back in as you walk around.

**Fixes.** Dungeon Info's Ctrl+Click (try on) and Shift+Click (link) now work. The Minimal chat's Combat Log tabs no longer cover its first lines, and it shows a loading bar like the game's. Dark Mode leaves the minimap's tracking and calendar icons bright (thanks, AntoineS). The pet and stance bars at a smaller Icon Size stop piling up on each other (thanks, Shumi).

**Housekeeping.** A /reload is enough after updating. Voice pack: still the one from 0.18.3.
