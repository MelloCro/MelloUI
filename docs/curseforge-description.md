# **MelloUI – An All-in-One UI for World of Warcraft: Forever**

_On your first login the installer sets MelloUI up with you, fitted to your screen. Type `/mello` for the settings any time._

## Install Questie too

**MelloUI's quests come from [Questie](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questie).** MelloUI keeps no quest data of its own any more. With Questie installed, its quests show on MelloUI's map and minimap in MelloUI's look, and the Quest List, Route to a quest and Dungeon Info's quests use them.

**Without Questie, no quests are shown.** The rest of MelloUI works as usual.

## MelloUI dresses the addons you already use

Your other addons do the work, MelloUI gives them its look and ties them into its map, tracker and route. They are all optional: install the ones you like and MelloUI finds them by itself. `/mello` > **Addons** lists each one, what MelloUI does with it, and a switch to leave it alone.

**Quests and leveling guides**

*   **[Questie](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questie):** quests on the map, the minimap, the Quest List and Route, in MelloUI's look. Its tracker follows the Quest Tracker's settings.
*   **[RestedXP Guide](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/restedxp-guide), [Zygor Guides](https://zygorguides.com), [Follow The Arrow](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/follow-the-arrow), [Azeroth Pilot Reloaded](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/azeroth-pilot-reloaded):** their steps on MelloUI's Quest Tracker, and Route leads you to each one.
*   **[Polestar](https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar), [FlowUp](https://flowup-wow.com), [TomTom](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/tomtom), [Guidelime](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/guidelime):** Route follows their steps, map pins or waypoints (Guidelime through TomTom's arrow).

**Map and minimap**

*   **[RareScanner](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/rarescanner):** rares, treasures and events on the map and minimap in MelloUI's icons, and the rares it finds on Rare Alert.
*   **[HandyNotes: NPCs (Forever)](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes-npcs-forever)** (with [HandyNotes](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes)): the auction house, the bank, trainers, mount sellers and more on the map and minimap, in MelloUI's icons.
*   **[GatherMate2](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/gathermate2):** gathering nodes on the map and the minimap, each ore and herb in its own MelloUI icon. **[HandyNotes](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes):** its notes on the map.
*   **[NodeSight](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/node-sight), [Lueur](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lueur):** the minimap stays in place while they scan for herbs and ore. Lueur's glow on the ore or herb you walk up to takes the place of MelloUI's gathering glints.

**Frames, bars and bags**

*   **[Clique](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/clique):** click-casting on MelloUI's unit and group frames.
*   **[MaxDPS](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/maxdps-rotation-helper):** spell recommendations on MelloUI's action bars.
*   **[Offhand](https://offhand-wow.onrender.com/):** with two screens, MelloUI's animations and notices stay on the game's screen.
*   **[Baganator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/baganator), [Bagnon](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/bagnon), [ArkInventory](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ark-inventory), [BetterBags](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/better-bags):** their bags in place of MelloUI's bag window, so B opens one bag window.
*   **[AHledger](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ahledger):** auction prices in the Discard button.
*   **[SharedMedia](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/sharedmedia):** its fonts, bar textures and sounds in MelloUI's lists.

## The look

*   **Three skins:** Default (the game's own look), Modern (MelloUI's own) and Oldschool (painted RPG art, a separate download).
*   **Fonts, bar textures, colours and icons** in one place, and **Dark Mode** for the whole UI.
*   **The game's windows** dressed to match, and a **Fader** that hides parts of the UI until you need them.

## Frames and bars

*   **Unit frames** for you, your target, focus and pet, with a designer: click a part, drag it.
*   **Party and raid frames** with indicators for heals, shields and debuffs.
*   **Frame effects:** heals, shields, cooldowns and buffs land on the frames, and a halo shows who has aggro.
*   **Personal Display:** your health, power and big cooldowns under your character, and a swing timer.
*   **Nameplates** with threat colours by role, combo points, crowd control, quest marks and your debuffs; every part movable in a designer. Class medallions over your party; glints on herbs, veins and quest items.
*   **Action bars, cast bar, XP and reputation bars,** and a **Level Up Card** with your gains and new spells at every level.
*   **Minimap** with a Services row (the nearest repair, inn, flight master and more) and one tidy group for other addons' buttons.
*   **Damage meter:** a race bar in fights, numbers on your frames and a fight history.

## Chat

*   A cleaner chat with channel tabs, chat buttons and a whisper window.
*   **Fake Link Warning:** a link dressed up as something else, like a Community invite written as an item, is marked FAKE LINK and opens nothing.

## Quests and travel

*   **A world map of its own:** one seamless map with your quests (from Questie), party, route, dungeons and other addons' pins.
*   **Quest Moments:** the game's yellow quest lines become small animations that fly to your quest in the tracker.
*   **Route** finds the quickest way over roads and flights; an arrow and a marker in the world lead you.
*   **Quest Tracker** and a **Quest List** of every quest near you (the Quest List needs Questie).
*   **Dungeon Info:** every dungeon's bosses, loot and drop rates, with maps of Forever's own dungeons. Click a dungeon's entrance on the map to open its page.
*   **Guide addons:** RestedXP, Zygor, Follow The Arrow, APR and TomTom steps show on the map, the marker and the tracker, with one arrow at a time.

## Quality of life

*   Auto repair and junk selling, **Restock** for your supplies, quests accepted and handed in for you.
*   **Reminders** for low supplies, mail, repairs, training and missing buffs.
*   Bags sorted by kind, item quality gems, a junk Discard button and cooldown numbers.
*   Combat text, gains beside your character, on-screen notices, quieter error messages, FPS and latency, and dark tooltips.

## Sound

*   **Voice Over:** quest givers read their text aloud, with subtitles.
*   **Custom Sounds** for clicks, windows and alerts, in each skin's style.

## And

*   **Edit Layout:** move and size anything, the game's Edit Mode parts too. Your layout follows your screen's shape.
*   **Profiles** to save, share and import your setup; each character keeps its own.
*   **Gamepad:** MelloUI's windows, map, bags and action bars work with a controller in the game's Gamepad mode.
*   The settings in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese and Korean.

## Install

1.  Install MelloUI with the CurseForge app. By hand: copy every folder from the zip into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`.
2.  **Install [Questie](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questie)** for quests, and any addon from the list above that you like.
3.  Restart the game.

**Voice pack (optional, 2.5 GB):** [part 1](https://github.com/MelloCro/MelloUI/releases/download/v0.18.3/MelloUI_VoicePack_Part1.zip) and [part 2](https://github.com/MelloCro/MelloUI/releases/download/v0.18.3/MelloUI_VoicePack_Part2.zip): unzip both into the same `AddOns` folder. Without it, the game's text-to-speech reads instead.
