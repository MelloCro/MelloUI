# MelloUI 0.13.4 — What's new (the hype version)

Screenshot slots in [brackets]. Say it like you'd say it on camera.

---

**Your whole UI just got painted.**
Every window. Every bar. The minimap, the nameplates, the tooltips, the chat, the damage meter,
even the settings window itself. Iron rails, gems, stone. One switch and it's all there. Don't
like it? Flip it off, the game looks like the game again, and everything else still works.
[before / after of the full screen in a fight] [character pane] [minimap + tracker] [chat + meter]

**Drag. Everything.**
Tick "Unlock the Windows" and just grab stuff. Windows, minimap, tracker, chat, damage meter.
The screen dims, a grid pops up, the border glows, it snaps to the centre lines, and the mouse
wheel resizes it while you hold it. Reload, restart, it's still where you put it. Works without
the reskin too.
[mid-drag with the grid and the glow] [the same window after a reload]

**One page to rule them all.**
UI Modifications. Top of the list, glowing, can't miss it. The reskin switch, one toggle per
area, and every tweak — nameplates, tooltips, chat, fonts, bar textures, class icons, cooldown
numbers, dark mode — as tabs on the same page. No more hunting.
[the Home page with the glowing tile] [the UI Modifications page]

**New here? It shows you around.**
First login: "painted look or the normal one?" then "want a tour?" The tour points at every
part of the window and tells you what it does. Skipped it? Tutorial button, or /mello tutorial.
[the two popups] [a tour step with the arrow]

**Every quest giver still talks — now the right one, every time.**
Voice Over waits for the quest window to actually load before it reads, so no more wrong quest
or silent NPC. Renumbered quests find their old recording. And we found out why so many vanilla
quests were silent: the voice pack never had them. We have the list. Recordings incoming.
[the overlay with an NPC talking]

**Small stuff that isn't small.**
Your settings finally stick (the backup was full, that's why things kept snapping back).
Bar textures can go back to Blizzard's, and your health colour beats the threat colour.
One font per job: text, chat, titles, damage. Dark Mode darkens the painted look too.
Action buttons don't get stuck pressed anymore. Plus a pile of fixes from going through the
whole addon line by line.
[/mello status] [Bar Textures tab next to a target frame] [Fonts tab]

**Gone:** the bottom bar. Nobody will miss it.
