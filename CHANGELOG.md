# Changelog

## 0.13.2

- Configuration window with its own button in the game menu; no Options > AddOns entry.
- Minimap stand: ring and legs around the map, the objective tracker chained to it.
- Services bar: round medallions, profession trainer button asks which profession.
- Map: click a boat, zeppelin or flight master to route there; Shift-click a dungeon or raid door.
- Bag slots dock under the bag window while the bag bar is hidden.
- Dungeon and raid entrances are learned on the way out as well as on the way in.
- Minimap coordinates hidden (Tweaks).
- Dark Mode shades the minimap stand and the medallion rims.
